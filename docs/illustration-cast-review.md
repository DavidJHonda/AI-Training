## Scope and approach

Visually reviewed contact sheets of 246 unique raster image paths referenced in the current local index.html, then inspected the three proposed pilots at full size. This is a local asset review, not verification of the deployed site or a video audit. The source includes an unused legacy component, noted below.

The replacement recommendations concern the repeated student-character treatment, not a verified identification of people from their faces. Keep the creators where text explicitly presents their work or personal examples. Use varied high school students, male and female, in general teaching scenes. Stars jerseys can remain a recurring visual detail.
