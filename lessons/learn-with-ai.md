# Learn with AI

Do you ever feel like there aren’t enough hours in the day? You might have a history quiz tomorrow, an essay due Friday, and what feels like a never-ending stream of algebra homework. Add sports, activities, family, and everything else, and finding enough time to study feels impossible.

There’s an easy solution: add four more hours to every day. Then you’d have plenty of time.

Of course, that isn’t possible. AI can’t add hours to your day, either.

## THE PATIENT TUTOR

Here’s the good news: AI might be the best study tool you’ll ever get your hands on. Used the right way, it helps you finish the work and makes you genuinely sharper at the subject.

Believe it or not, this is one place where a machine has an advantage over a human tutor. AI will quiz you at 1 a.m., never sigh when you ask again, and try another explanation when you’re stuck.

You can always chat with Gemini, Claude, or ChatGPT to learn something new. But some AI tools are built specifically to help you study, whether you’re working from class materials or working through something you don’t understand.

### Board 1: Which Study Tool for the Job?

**Image file:** `learn-with-ai-study-toolkit.jpg`

![Which Study Tool for the Job?](learn-with-ai-study-toolkit.jpg)

**Teaching content:**

Here’s how to choose the right study tool for the job.

Your study tool is Gemini Notebook. It uses your materials to answer questions and create study tools. That keeps your exam prep focused on what you need to learn. Its best use is when you have the materials the test covers, including class notes, screenshots, study guides, a webpage, or a YouTube video.

Your learning coach is ChatGPT’s Study mode or Gemini’s Guided Learning. It guides you through a topic with questions, hints, and explanations. You do the thinking, one step at a time. Its best use is when you want help with a difficult concept, a different explanation, or practice that checks your understanding.

Check your class materials. Ask your teacher if something doesn’t line up.

## YOUR STUDY TOOL

Use Gemini Notebook as your main study tool when you’re studying from class materials. It keeps your work grounded in the notes, slides, and sources your teacher gave you.

### Board 3: Your Four Moves for Gemini Notebook

**Image file:** `learn-with-ai-four-moves-faceless.jpg`

![Your Four Moves for Gemini Notebook](learn-with-ai-four-moves-faceless.jpg)

**Teaching content:**

Here are your four moves for Gemini Notebook.

One subject per notebook: mixing subjects is like asking, “Quiz me on history, but make some of the questions about math.”

Give it the full picture: add your notes, slides, videos, and anything else you need to learn.

Quiz yourself blind: tell Gemini Notebook, “Generate a 10-question quiz from these sources only. Don’t show the answers yet. Wait for mine, then grade me.”

Trace it back: if something is unclear, click the citation. It takes you back to the original material.

## YOUR LEARNING COACH

Sometimes you don’t need another study guide. You need help understanding something that hasn’t clicked.

ChatGPT’s Study mode and Gemini’s Guided Learning can walk you through a topic with questions, hints, and explanations. Tell it what you’re learning and where you’re stuck. Try answering its questions, ask for a hint when you need one, and request a different explanation if you’re still confused.

You do the thinking. AI helps you figure out your next step.
