# Welcome Course Arc — Personality Artwork Prompt

Mode: built-in image generation with local identity references

Use case: identity-preserve

Asset type: supporting character artwork for a 1600×900 educational course roadmap
board

Input images: `illustrations/welcome.jpg` and
`illustrations/does-school-matter.jpg` were used as identity and uniform references.
Luke has light-brown tight curls and green hockey jersey 4. Nate has dark curls and
green hockey jersey 96.

Primary request: Reimagine Luke and Nate as simplified contemporary editorial/vector
characters in one wide horizontal strip containing exactly five equally sized,
isolated scenes:

1. Work — Luke productively uses an AI assistant at a laptop.
2. Understand — Nate examines an open-hood AI mechanism with visible layers and
   learned patterns.
3. Avoid — Luke uses a magnifying glass and shield to catch a misleading AI answer.
4. Embrace — Nate confidently faces a changing future horizon and upward path.
5. Build — Luke and Nate assemble practical blocks into a rising staircase.

Style and composition: clean, friendly educational vector illustration; simplified
faces and anatomy while preserving each host’s hair, youthful appearance, and jersey
number. Exactly five equal columns, one centered scene per column, one person in each
of the first four scenes and both hosts in the fifth, consistent scale, generous
white space, and separable scenes for vertical cropping.

Color palette: forest green, black, white, dark navy, course purple, and restrained
blue, orange, teal, and pale lavender.

Constraints: simplified green/black/white hockey uniforms inspired by the references
but no exact team logos, league logos, trademarks, or brand marks. No words or labels
other than the correct jersey numerals 4 and 96. White background; no headings,
captions, borders, dividers, cards, watermarks, extra people, mascot, hockey sticks,
pucks, rink, or sports-action metaphors. The educational action—not hockey—remains
the point of every scene.
