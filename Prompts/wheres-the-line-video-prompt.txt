After the board, develop the ethical reasoning without repeating the definition. Explain why accuracy, profitability, usefulness, laws, and company policies do not settle every responsible-use decision. Responsibility belongs to people who choose, build, buy, approve, and operate AI systems, including people who never write a model.

Teach the four actions on Making the Responsible Choice in order: consider everyone affected; be clear with people; build in protection; own the outcome. Explain each action with the board’s concrete question or behavior. Then apply all four briefly to the DraftKings story: consider vulnerable customers, explain offers honestly, evaluate safeguards, and monitor consequences. Do not present an untested technical fix as guaranteed.

Explain that reasonable people may disagree, but strong reasoning accounts for affected people, evidence, and consequences. Do not narrate the fictional TRY IT scenarios.
