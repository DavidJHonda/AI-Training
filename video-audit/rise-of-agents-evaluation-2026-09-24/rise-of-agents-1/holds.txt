== HOLD 1:28.37 -> 2:11.57  (43.2s)
   narration: The innovation is entirely about what happens after you hit submit. Instead of answering once and stopping, an agent operates on a continuous four-step feedback loop. Step one is the goal. You state clearly what you want to achieve. Step two is the plan. The agent breaks that goal down into actionable steps. Step three is the act phase, where the agent uses specific tools to complete the next step. Step four is check. It looks at the result and evaluates it against your original goal. Then it asks itself, done or not? Not done? Go again. The AI feeds its own evaluation right back into the planning stage to figure out its next move. An agent loops until the goal is met. You set the goal and judge the result.
