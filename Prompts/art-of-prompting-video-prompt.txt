Focus: a person meets your question halfway — a teacher knows what class you're in, a friend asks a follow-up — but AI answers exactly the words you typed. Then image 1, the four qualities of a good question — nothing about AI changes them — and walk each quality WITH its one-line gloss spoken aloud: open-minded, you haven't picked the answer in advance; specific, it asks for exactly what you need; on target, it asks about the right thing; open-ended, it leaves room for an answer you didn't expect. Packaging comes down to three moves. Image 3, move two — describe the answer you want: the model fills in every blank you leave, so describe the target — the shape, the limits, an example to match, who AI should be; walk the lacrosse-caption bad/better pair. Then the dial: you don't need all of these every time — a quick factual question needs none, real work takes moves one and two, a big project adds steps.
