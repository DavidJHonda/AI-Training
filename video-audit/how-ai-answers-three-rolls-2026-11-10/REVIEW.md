### Roll 3

- **Strength:** at 0:20.74–0:28.40 it states the question-plus-generated-text context clearly. The two-context comparison and choice variation also survive.
- **0:46.30–0:51.44:** “before inference actually begins” places prompt processing outside inference. This conflicts with its own broad closing definition and the standard prefill/decode account.
- **1:06.64–1:11.98:** starting vectors carry “the baseline dictionary definition of each word.” Learned embeddings are not dictionary-definition records.
- **1:39.18–1:43.92:** “exclusively” previous tokens incorrectly excludes the current token’s own representation and computation.
- **2:06.10–2:14.52:** “mathematical weight” and “dictated entirely” blur contextual activations with model weights and overstate the role of preceding text.
- **3:09.66–3:16.42:** a token creates “a brand new context window.” It changes the sequence in context; it does not create a fresh window capacity.
- **3:24.88–3:31.44:** “permanent part of the context” is not a safe general claim. Context is bounded, and some architectures use sliding windows. [Hugging Face cache documentation](https://huggingface.co/docs/transformers/v5.15.0/kv_cache)
- **3:39.88–3:51.44:** the spoken dog-name distribution omits the 47% remainder.
- **4:00.80–4:08.82:** “never retrieved intact” and “grown organically” are unnecessary absolute/metaphorical additions.
- All seven required sentences are paraphrased. Repairing this roll would require replacing several explanations and the close. There is no auditioned repair plan; use roll 2 instead.
- Visuals also invent 42%/28%/16%/8% dog-name odds around 3:36 and a 98.2% stop probability near 4:24. These are separate editing problems, not the basis for rejecting its narration.
