Create a complete video lesson from unexpected-results.md. Follow its wording and order. The Hanoi Rat Story is the main example of unexpected results; Text Messaging and Cane Toads are two further examples.

TEACH THE COMPLETE LESSON
Briefly introduce confident opinions about AI’s future, then tell the Hanoi story in full. Preserve its sequence, concrete details and dry humor: 1902, rats in the new sewers, payment for rat tails, thousands of tails, then tailless rats. Explain immediately that surviving rats could keep breeding, so hunters clipped tails and released the rats. Give the pays-once line, rat farms, and the ending: a mountain of tails, but more rats. Explain how paying for tails worked as designed while defeating the goal of fewer rats. Land the unexpected-result sentence. This is the central story, not merely a warm-up about rewards. Use the exact bridge to two further unexpected results. Text Messaging: short messages over mobile networks became much more popular than their creators expected; young people helped make texting an everyday habit. Cane Toads: imported to control sugarcane pests, they barely controlled them, poisoned native animals and spread. Teach both outcomes aloud. Connect all three cases to AI: its most important effects may be unpredicted, good or bad. Keep curiosity, recognizing uncertainty, watching what people actually do, building durable skills, and the closing lines. Do not force all three cases into the same explanation about rewards. No GPS, highways, traffic explanation, extra examples or predictions about which AI camp is right.

VOICE
