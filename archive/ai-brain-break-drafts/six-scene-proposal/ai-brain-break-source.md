# AI Brain Break: A Very Confident Explanation

This is a comedy script. All six claims below are deliberately false. They are jokes, not factual teaching.

## 1. Mind Reading

AI reads your mind through your cell phone. That’s how its answers sound so personal. The phone in your pocket picks up your thoughts and sends them straight to the model. Forget typing. Just think louder.

## 2. Tokens

AI tokens are arcade coins. Every time you ask a question, a tiny robot drops one into a machine. Long questions need a whole bucket of coins. This is why AI companies are always asking for more tokens.

## 3. Training

Training an AI means making it run laps around a data center. The more laps it runs, the smarter it gets. The best models train before breakfast. Nobody wants an AI that gets tired halfway through a homework question.

## 4. Layers

AI adds layers when it gets cold. Bigger models need bigger jackets. Engineers keep data centers cool, so models need plenty of layers. A hundred-layer model has a very crowded closet.

## 5. Attention

Attention is how AI stays awake late at night, even after its bedtime. Without attention, the model would fall asleep before finishing your answer. That is why it can help you at two in the morning.

## 6. Claude-A-Roni

ChatGPT and Gemini run on computer chips, but Claude runs on a special type of pasta called Claude-A-Roni. It is a proprietary rotini developed for advanced reasoning. For difficult questions, simply add more sauce.

## Closing line

Now you know how AI really works. Please do not put pasta in your computer.
