## Selection and proposed edit

**Version 5 — repair.** The existing audio supplies the accurate statistical distinction. The opening art and timing need an edit. Retain the hook, both reactions, Bohannon’s purpose, the knowledge board, and the close from this take. No pedagogical benefit justifies swapping narrators for a closer wording match on those beats.

**Version 4 — pass over as the base.** Its opening sentence and first-impression bridge are strong. Using it would require replacing the claims that the study and research were fabricated, removing the mathematical guarantee, and cutting the “permanent mental shield” aside. The generated “Miracle Diet Cocoa Craze” artwork also substitutes a different headline under the Shape narration.

**Version 6 — pass over as the base.** Its compact opening and habit transition work. The premature verdict on the skeptical student, the repeated certainty claims, and the formula-heavy graphic around 1:30 make this the most extensive repair.
