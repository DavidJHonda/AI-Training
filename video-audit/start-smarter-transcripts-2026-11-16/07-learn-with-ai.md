# 7. Learn with AI

**[0:00]** You know the feeling. You have a history quiz tomorrow, a rough draft of an essay due Friday, and a tall stack of algebra homework. Once you add in sports, clubs, and family time, finding enough hours in the day to actually study feels impossible. The easiest fix would be adding four more hours to the day. But since we can't do that, and AI can't magically create time either, we have to look at what AI can actually do for you. Used the right way, it is the most effective study tool you can get your hands on. It helps you get through the work while making sure you understand the material. This is one specific area where a machine has a clear advantage over a human tutor. An AI will patiently quiz you at one in the morning. It never sighs in frustration when you ask a question for the third time. And it will always try a new explanation when you're stuck.

**[0:48]** You can always open up a standard chat with Gemiini or ChatGPT to learn something new. But to really master your classes, you need to use AI tools built specifically for studying, rather than just general chatting. This board lays out how to choose the right study tool for the job. You have two main paths to choose from depending on what you need to accomplish. The first path is your study tool, like Gemini Notebook. It uses your own materials to answer questions and create study aids. This keeps your exam prep tightly focused on the facts you need to learn. It is best used when you already have the exact materials the test covers.

**[1:24]** The second path is your learning coach, like ChatGPT's Study Mode or Gemini's Guided Learning. This tool guides you step-by-step through a complex concept with hints and questions. It leaves the actual thinking to you. Whichever tool you use, always verify the output. Check your class materials. Ask your teacher if something doesn't line up. Let's focus entirely on the study tool track. When you prepare for a specific test, you have to ground your AI session in reality. That means pulling together the actual notes, slides, and sources your teacher gave you. This graphic shows the input process for Gemini Nearbook. On the left side, you upload your materials. You can provide your notes, PDFs, slide decks, images, websites, and even YouTube videos.

**[2:10]** From those inputs, you get back custom quizzes, flashcards, study guides, audio and video overviews, mind maps, and more. Turn your class materials into the study tools you need. This infographic lays out four specific operational moves required to use Gemini Notebook effectively.

**[3:16]** This is where you switch to the learning coach track. State the topic and pinpoint exactly where you are having difficulty.

**[3:23]** Then attempt to answer the questions it asks you. Request a hint when you need one. Or ask for an alternate explanation if you are still stuck. The core rule here is strict. You do the thinking. AI helps you figure out your next step. This board summarizes the framework. Use AI to learn, not to skip the learning. Practice, ask questions, explain it yourself.
