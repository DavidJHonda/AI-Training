## NAVIGATE THE FUTURE

# Work Changes

Before you know it, you’ll be finished with school and starting your career. There’s lots of talk these days about how AI is changing jobs. What might that mean for you?

AI can change a job by changing the tasks within it. An entrepreneur, teacher, or lawyer might keep the same job title while doing very different work each day.

Keep two ideas in mind. First, you already know AI is especially useful for certain kinds of tasks. Those are good places to start using it at work. Second, what will set you apart is what you already know.

Now put those ideas together on your first assignment at work, and see how the work changes with AI.

### Board 1: Your First Assignment

**Image file:** `work-changes-assignment-faceless.jpg`

![Your First Assignment](work-changes-assignment-faceless.jpg)

**Teaching content:**

Your first assignment.

Your manager tells you:

“We’re getting bad customer feedback on our new product. Read the last 500 reviews and tell us what’s happening and how we should fix it.”

**Stage 1: Routine Work.**

Learn what customers are saying.

**Before AI:** You read all 500 reviews, group the complaints, build the spreadsheet, and write a summary.

**With AI:** AI organizes the reviews and drafts a summary. You spot-check the results and investigate anything that looks wrong.

**Stage 2: Judgment & Decisions.**

Decide what to do about it.

**Before AI:** You investigate the causes, compare fixes, and present your recommendation.

**With AI:** AI helps you explore causes and compare fixes. You evaluate its suggestions and present your recommendation.

Same assignment. Same responsibility. A different way to do the work.

## What Changes

Before AI, you read and organized the reviews yourself.

With AI, you get help organizing the feedback and drafting a summary.

You spot-check the results, investigate the problems, and decide what to recommend.

Less time on routine work can mean more time for judgment and decisions.

The more you know, the better you’ll be at that work.

## The Concepts

When people talk about how AI is changing jobs, you’ll hear two terms: automate and augment.

### Board 2: Two Ways AI Changes Work

**Image file:** `work-changes-automation-and-augmentation.jpg`

![Two Ways AI Changes Work](work-changes-automation-and-augmentation.jpg)

**Teaching content:**

Two ways AI changes work.

Automate. AI takes over a step. It sorted the reviews, grouped the ideas, and created the first summary.

Augment. AI helps a person do more. AI helped you explore more explanations, compare more options, and improve your recommendation.

The work still has your name on it. You own the outcome.

## A New Problem

AI also creates a new challenge for people starting their careers. In many professions, the grunt work has been a valuable training ground.

In our example, reading the reviews helps you learn how customers think, what they care about, and how they describe their experiences with the product. If AI does most of that reading for you, how will you build that understanding?

Here’s the risk:

AI can make you productive before it makes you knowledgeable.

You might be asked to investigate a problem and recommend what to do before you understand the field well enough to judge AI’s suggestions.

What can you do? Use some of the time AI saves to build your own understanding. In our example, read a sample of the reviews and write down what you notice before looking at AI’s summary. Then compare: what did you miss, and what did AI miss?

What can you do today? Learn. And learn more. And you’re in the best place in the world to do it: school.

## Closing Message

AI is changing the work you’ll do.

Now it’s on you to LEARN.
