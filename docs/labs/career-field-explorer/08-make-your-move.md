# Lab 8: Finish something worth keeping

**Lesson:** Make Your Move  
**Time:** 12–15 minutes  
**Starter:** `07-be-curious-recovery.html` or the student's working file  
**Recovery:** `08-make-your-move-recovery.html`  
**Reference:** `reference.html`

## Build

Finish the app with local autosave, export, print, reset, responsive polish, and a
clear saved-state message. Keep the data on the device. Then replace example content
with three fields the student genuinely wants to explore and choose one next action.

## Prompt to use with AI

> Finish this offline app without adding a server, account, analytics, package, or
> external dependency. Save edits in localStorage and show a clear saved-state
> message. Add Export to a readable text file, Print, and Reset with confirmation.
> Preserve accessibility and make the full page work at 390 pixels wide. Do not
> change the rule that the app never ranks fields or recommends a career. After
> coding, list the exact behaviors I should test.

## Test before finishing

- Edits survive a page reload.
- Export creates a readable file containing priorities, fields, sources, comparison,
  selected field, question, and next action.
- Reset asks before erasing work.
- Print removes editing controls and keeps the content readable.
- The app has no horizontal scrolling at 390 pixels wide.
- The student chooses and completes one real next action within the next week.

## What this lab teaches

A prompt is not proof of skill. A working file, tested behavior, sourced content,
human feedback, and a real next move are proof.
