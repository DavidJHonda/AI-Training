## Secondary items for the next editing pass

These do not justify new lessons or a larger course:

- **Training Bias / Document Trap:** RAG is explained in both consecutive lessons. Keep a short lookup bridge in Training Bias and let Document Trap carry the mechanism. Stale information is already correctly labeled a separate limitation.
- **Tokens:** retain the stated tokenizer context. Exact segmentation and IDs should remain illustrations, not general facts students must memorize.
- **Brain Break written feedback:** “AI doesn’t hear anything. It reads the text you type” is too broad for a course that also discusses audio. Reject the joke without adding this inaccurate generalization. No video change was evaluated.
- **AI is Math:** state the equal-likelihood assumption for the simple counting formula. That small qualification is enough for the fair-coin example.
- **Big Upside lab:** keep “predicted structure” visible when describing the database result. The live teaching already makes this distinction well.
- **Final review links and tags:** fx25 is the clearest wrong destination. Review the remaining links for the best explanatory destination, and synchronize old section labels with the current map. For example, the question about sampling variation could point more directly to One More Thing.
- **Lab time estimates:** validate with students using the intended account types. Do not infer completion time from a step count or from how quickly an experienced adult can follow the instructions.

The next useful evidence is a student’s explanation and transfer attempt after a lesson. A longer editorial checklist would not replace that evidence.
