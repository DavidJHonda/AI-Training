Create a 3:30–4:00 video overview of the attached “Work Changes” lesson. The Markdown is the authority. Use the five attached boards in order and hold each while narrating it.

Boards: 1 four AI strengths; 2 the same assignment before and with AI; 3 automate versus augment; 4 what changes for workers; 5 close.

Core idea: job titles may remain, but the work underneath changes. The first pass becomes the starting line.

Open on students entering careers. Most will not have an “AI job.” They will become nurses, engineers, marketers, electricians, teachers, lawyers, and designers who use AI inside the job.

Board 1: briefly walk the four shapes of work AI handles well: transform, generate, compress, and reason. What a worker already knows still determines what they ask and what they do with the answer.

Board 2: the boss asks a new employee to analyze 500 customer comments. Before AI, most time goes to reading, grouping, spreadsheets, and drafting a presentation. With AI, the first pass arrives quickly. The employee checks source comments, finds what the summary missed, investigates why, and recommends what the company should do.

Board 3: explain automate vs augment. Automation removes or compresses a step. Augmentation helps a person explore more and produce a stronger result. Most jobs will contain both, and a person still owns the outcome.

Board 4: walk all three changes: more kinds of work, greater productivity, and more meaningful work. Then explain the first rung: beginner work is often what AI handles first, so young workers must deliberately build knowledge and practice. Required narration: “AI can make you productive before it makes you knowledgeable.”

End on board 5. Speak exactly: “AI is changing the work you’ll do.” “Now it’s on you to learn.” Board 5 remains visible through the final frame.
