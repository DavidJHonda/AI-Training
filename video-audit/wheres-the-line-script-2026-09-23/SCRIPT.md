# Where's the Line? — narration script with insertion marks (2026-09-23)

The narration is the Markdown, read in order. Times are estimates at Notebook's pace (about 3:25
total; the roll will differ). Marks in brackets are ours, added in the edit: when a board comes in,
which card rings, and when we cut back to Notebook's drawing. Everything without a mark is
Notebook's own drawn scene under the narration. VERBATIM lines are enforced by the prompt.

Applies to both rolls. In the board-upload roll (`wheres-the-line`) Notebook also shows the two
boards itself; we replace them and still cut away on the marks below.

---

**0:00** AI can do remarkable things. But, to borrow a line from a famous document, VERBATIM "We the people are responsible for how we use it."

**0:10** That responsibility goes beyond writing better prompts and evaluating AI's answers. It includes deciding what we ask AI to do and considering who could be affected.

**0:20** There's a word for this you've heard: ethics. It means thinking through what you should do and how your decisions affect other people.

**0:30** DraftKings is an online gambling platform where customers play casino games and make sports bets. VERBATIM "The company earns money when customers lose."

**0:39** This business model isn't unique to DraftKings. Traditional casinos work the same way. But some people struggle to control their gambling. Gambling addiction is a recognized mental health condition that can seriously affect finances, relationships, and health.

**0:54** Customers leave a digital record of their betting activity when they use DraftKings. AI can analyze that record to identify patterns in how often they play, how much they bet, and how much they lose.

**1:08** Here's a thought. What if DraftKings could use AI to predict which customers would gamble and lose more after receiving a special offer? It could send those customers a promotional email like VERBATIM "Place a bet today and get bonus bets."

**1:24** Here's another thought. What if they could use AI to identify people who might be developing a gambling problem and offer help instead of a promotion?

**1:34** This creates an ethical question: VERBATIM "When should DraftKings stop encouraging a customer to play if continued gambling could harm them?"

**1:43** The New York Times investigated these choices at DraftKings. Here's what it reported.

**1:48 [BOARD 1 How DraftKings Uses AI — full view, no ring]** DraftKings developed AI for two different purposes.

**1:54 [ring Targeted Promotions card → ring its What They Did]** Targeted Promotions: DraftKings developed AI to analyze betting habits, account balances, and losses to predict which customers would gamble and lose more after receiving free bets and bonuses. What they did: DraftKings put the technology into use and continued developing it.

**2:10 [ring Customer Protection card → ring its What They Did]** Customer Protection: employees developed AI to analyze betting patterns and identify people who might be developing a gambling problem, so the company could intervene earlier. What they did: DraftKings chose not to put this predictive technology into use.

**2:25 [back to Notebook's drawing]** DraftKings disputes targeting customers based on losses. It says it already monitors risky behavior and didn't find enough evidence that the proposed predictive system would help.

**2:36** AI can do what it's asked while still harming people. Here are four moves to help you make responsible choices.

**2:45 [BOARD 2 Making the Responsible Choice — full view, then dive to Consider Everyone Affected, purple ring]** Consider everyone affected: look beyond the people who benefit. Who could be harmed, excluded, or pressured?

**2:52 [pan to Be Clear With People, blue ring]** Be clear with people: explain what the system does and how it affects their choices.

**2:58 [pan to Build In Protection, teal ring]** Build in protection: test for harm, set boundaries, and give people a way to challenge mistakes.

**3:05 [pan to Own The Outcome, amber ring]** Own the outcome: monitor what happens. Change or stop the system when the consequences warrant it.

**3:12 [pull back to full board, no ring]** VERBATIM "A responsible decision considers the people who live with it."

**3:16 [STANDARD CLOSE]** VERBATIM "We the People Make the Call." VERBATIM "What should AI do? That's our responsibility."
