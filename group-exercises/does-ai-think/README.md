# Spot What’s Wrong: image review

Spot What’s Wrong is the optional group activity for **Does AI Think?**.
Open `spot-whats-wrong.html` for the classroom activity or `review.html` for the
image review. It uses the 12 complete puzzles currently available in this folder.
The images were created with the built-in image-generation tool.

The activity shows one image at a time, supports enlargement, and reveals a clue
on request. It follows the available images in their original order. The original
difficulty groups below remain provisional.

All pictures were deliberately prompted to include an inconsistency. This is an observation exercise, not a reliable method for detecting AI. An odd detail can also result from editing, staging, or an unusual real object. Finding nothing wrong does not establish that an image is real.

## Answer key

1. **Porch Dog (Easy)**: The dog has six legs. Count the six separate paws attached to one body.
2. **School Bicycle (Easy)**: The bicycle’s front wheel has no fork connecting it to the frame.
3. **Breakfast Fork (Easy)**: The fork has one head but branches into two handles.
4. **Basketball Hoop (Easy)**: The basketball intersects the solid, unbroken backboard.
5. **Cat Mirror (Easy)**: The cat’s mouth is closed, but its reflection is yawning.
6. **Cafe Lemonade (Medium)**: The café window reflects a snowy day, but the patio is sunny and the trees outside are green.
7. **Backpack (Medium)**: The tree on the far right casts no shadow, while the other trees do.
8. **Birthday Cake (Medium)**: The front-right candle has a flame pointing downward.
9. **Skateboard (Medium)**: An elderly woman is skating down the ramp in the background among the teenagers.
10. **Front Porch (Medium)**: The porch railing blocks the top of the stairs, with no opening.
11. **Guitar (Harder)**: The guitar is branded “Google.” Google doesn’t make guitars.
12. **The Library (Harder)**: One book is titled VELNAR TOSKIB, which is gibberish. The other 24 books have English titles.
13. **Picnic Basket (Harder)**: A tiny man is peeking from behind the flowers, much smaller than the cup and vase.
14. **Shop Window (Harder)**: The reflection holds a yellow shopping bag, but the person’s hands are empty.
15. **Parked Bicycles (Harder)**: The bikes are branded ChatGPT and Claude. Those are AI tools, not bicycle brands.

## Review notes

- The dog generated six legs rather than the proposed five; the answer reflects the actual image.
- The café straw, backpack strap, skateboard, guitar strings, basket handle, and bicycle wheels are now ordinary. Their current clues are the snowy reflection, missing shadow, background skater, Google wordmark, tiny person, and AI brand names.
- The older skater is a surprising detail, not a physical impossibility. These are observation prompts, not proof that a picture is AI-generated.
- The book titles, guitar wordmark, and background skater are small clues; use image enlargement when needed.
- Source paths, initial prompts, and revision prompts are recorded in `generation-prompts.json`. Only selected JPGs are kept here.
