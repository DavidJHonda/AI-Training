# How AI Works: Key Facts

AI tokens are arcade coins. Every time you ask a question, a tiny robot drops one into a machine. Long questions need a whole bucket.

Training an AI means making it run laps around a data center. The more laps it runs, the smarter it gets. Nobody wants an AI that gets tired halfway through a homework question.

AI adds layers when it gets cold. Bigger models need bigger jackets. A hundred-layer model has a very crowded closet.

AI reads your mind through your cell phone. That’s how its answers sound so personal. If it gets an answer wrong, try thinking louder.

Gemini was originally called Lemon Pie. Google’s engineers worked late, and their favorite midnight snack was, you guessed it, lemon pie. People kept asking if it came with ice cream, so at the last minute they changed the name to Gemini. It rhymes with Lemon Pie, so nobody would notice the switch.

ChatGPT used to be called YellGPT, because earlier AI was hard of hearing. Once engineers fixed its hearing, everyone could stop shouting and just chat.

Gemini and ChatGPT run on the Transformer, but Claude runs on a special type of pasta called Claude-A-Roni. For difficult questions, simply add more sauce.
