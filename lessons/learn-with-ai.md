# Learn with AI

Do you ever feel like there aren’t enough hours in the day? You might have a history quiz tomorrow, an essay due Friday, and what feels like a never-ending stream of algebra homework. Add sports, activities, family, and everything else, and finding enough time to study feels impossible.

There’s an easy solution: add four more hours to every day. Then you’d have plenty of time.

Of course, that isn’t possible. AI can’t add hours to your day, either.

## THE PATIENT TUTOR

Here’s the good news: AI might be the best study tool you’ll ever get your hands on. Used the right way, it helps you finish the work and makes you genuinely sharper at the subject.

Believe it or not, this is one place where a machine has an advantage over a human tutor. AI will quiz you at 1 a.m., never sigh when you ask again, and meet you exactly where you’re stuck.

The first move is choosing which kind of AI to aim at the job. There’s a simple question to guide your choice: do you want to learn from materials you already have, or learn something new?

### Board 1: Which Study Tool for the Job?

**Image file:** `learn-with-ai-1-study-tools.jpg`

![Which Study Tool for the Job?](learn-with-ai-1-study-tools.jpg)

**Teaching content:**

Focus, which is Gemini Notebook: it uses your materials to answer questions and create study tools. That keeps your exam prep focused on what you need to learn. Best use: you have the materials the test covers, including class notes, screenshots, study guides, a webpage, or a YouTube video. The catch: if your notes are missing a key concept, it cannot reliably fill the gap.

Exploration, which is ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini: it makes things from scratch. Trained on massive amounts of data, it can explain or quiz you on almost anything. Best use: you do not have the materials, or you want a concept explained a new way or extra practice your notes do not cover. The catch: it can make things up or explain a concept differently from how your teacher taught it and expects it on the exam.

Choose the tool that matches how you need to learn.

## WHY GEMINI NOTEBOOK

Use Gemini Notebook as your main study tool when you’re studying from class materials. It keeps your work grounded in the notes, slides, and sources your teacher gave you.

### Board 2: How Gemini Notebook Works

**Image file:** `learn-with-ai-2-how-it-works.jpg`

![How Gemini Notebook Works](learn-with-ai-2-how-it-works.jpg)

**Teaching content:**

You upload: your notes and PDFs, slides, images, websites and articles, YouTube videos, and more. You get: quizzes, flashcards, study guides, audio and video overviews, mind maps, and more.

Turn your class materials into the study tools you need.

### Board 3: Your Four Moves for Gemini Notebook

**Image file:** `learn-with-ai-3-four-moves.jpg`

![Your Four Moves for Gemini Notebook](learn-with-ai-3-four-moves.jpg)

**Teaching content:**

One subject per notebook: mixing subjects is like asking, “Quiz me on history, but make some of the questions about math.”

Give it the full picture: add your notes, slides, videos, and anything else you need to learn.

Quiz yourself blind: tell Gemini Notebook, “Generate a 10-question quiz from these sources only. Don’t show the answers yet. Wait for mine, then grade me.”

Trace it back: if something is unclear, click the citation. It takes you back to the original material.

Use AI to strengthen the learning, not skip it.
