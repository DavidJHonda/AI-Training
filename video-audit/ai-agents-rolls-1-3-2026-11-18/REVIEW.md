# AI Agents · three-roll review

**Use roll 3 as the base.** It gets a student from a familiar chat to a concrete job in 14 seconds. The course builders describe the layout in plain English, the agent finds the file and tests the change, and the people decide whether the lesson works for students. Roll 3 also asks what happens to workers without predicting who will keep a job. Use roll 1 for the four application stories and roll 2 for the agent loop. The resulting lesson would run roughly 3:15–3:20, depending on the joins. The available narration covers the lesson without a fourth generation.

**Review basis.** I compared the current page, lesson Markdown, reroll prompt, and three course boards with complete time-aligned transcripts of the three November uploads. Sources and hashes are in [sources.json](sources.json). Quotes below are excerpts from local speech recognition, with capitalization normalized in places. I treated paraphrases as successful when they conveyed the meaning. The September files with similar names are different recordings. I inspected frames every four seconds in all three uploads and the installed video, enlarged selected frames, and measured silence near the proposed edits. This is a content and sampled-picture review. I could not directly listen to delivery or audition the joins, so the splice recommendations are provisional. The timecodes are source positions.
