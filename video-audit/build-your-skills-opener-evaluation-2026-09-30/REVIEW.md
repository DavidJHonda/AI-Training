## Findings

1. **The September 26 repair solved the major coverage gap.** At 1:39–1:51, the narration now explains collaboration and creative thinking. All three map rows have meaningful spoken coverage. Do not repeat the September 24 finding that row two is absent.
2. **The narration is too formal in several important places.** At 0:16–0:26, “technical operation to human durability” and “cement the instincts” obscure a simple introduction. At 1:33–1:39, “we pivot to skills whose value actively appreciates over time” is harder than “skills that grow in value.” At 2:10–2:13, “overarching mandate” delays an already strong takeaway. These phrases make a student-facing opener sound like a corporate presentation.
3. **Required wording remains unresolved.** The creed expands “Your questions” and “Your skills”; “Build Your Skills” is not said; all three map rows are paraphrased rather than read as the current prompt requires; and the reflection question becomes “What do you bring to the equation that it entirely lacks?” Meaning generally survives, but the intended recurring language does not.
4. **The map is the main pacing opportunity.** It stays on screen from 1:13.00 to 2:17.23, a 64.23-second continuous board run, roughly 39% of the video. The full-row highlights help. This hold was explicitly approved in September 26's edit, so it is not a newly discovered violation of that approval. For a future version, plainer narration should shorten the walk before adding more pictures or camera movement.
5. **The bike passage and ending work on the evidence reviewed.** The story explains that equipment changes while a learned skill remains, transfers to other activities, and must be built through practice. The two closing lines are correct and have no narration after them. The sampled close frames match the current design and show the prescribed push progressing toward a settled hold.
