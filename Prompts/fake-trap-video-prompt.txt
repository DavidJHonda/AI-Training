Then name the trap with the lesson's own line: the Fake Trap is believing it because it looks real — and it has a second jaw, dismissing the truth because it could be a fake. Cruelty: some fakes exist to humiliate one person, and that's the version most likely to show up at your school. Then the detector dead end: a detector is one more AI making one more prediction, and the fakes improve faster than it does — treat its verdict as a clue, never a ruling. Then the one rule under all three, spoken exactly: verify somewhere the sender doesn't control. Until a second, independent source shows the same thing, unverified is your answer. Your eyes still work fine for enjoying what you know is made up; they just stopped working as a lie detector.
