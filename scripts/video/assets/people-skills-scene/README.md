## Generation prompt

Create an illustration-only source sheet for an educational lesson about people skills for high-school students. 2 by 2 grid, four equally sized rectangular panels, overall landscape 3:2 aspect ratio. No text, lettering, captions, speech bubbles, logos, or panel borders. Polished contemporary flat editorial cartoon illustration, softly shaded solid shapes, expressive natural faces, absolutely no photos or photorealism. Consistent cast in all four panels: Maya, a teenage girl with medium brown skin and dark curly shoulder-length hair wearing a purple sweatshirt; a teenage boy with short brown hair wearing teal; and another teenage student wearing blue. They are teammates sitting around a classroom project table with a laptop, presentation sheets, and an assignment. Keep all faces/hands/action within central 70% of each panel and use generous background breathing space. Upper left lavender background: Maya is explaining an idea with an open hand, the teal-shirted boy listens attentively, laptop between them. Upper right pale blue background: Maya has gone quiet, looking down toward the assignment, and the boy gently invites her to speak with a relaxed open hand, no touching or distressed caricature. Lower left pale teal background: boy gives Maya credit by gesturing appreciatively toward her as she points to the assignment and third student listens. Lower right pale warm cream background: Maya and boy calmly compare a printed assignment with their laptop presentation, each indicating the work, respectful disagreement, no aggressive gestures. Same clothing and faces throughout. High-quality meaningful classroom scenes, mature enough for teenagers, no robot or decorative symbols.

## Notebook uploads

Visible faces are never uploaded. `render_people_skills_uploads.py` builds same-size text-only stand-ins for Your Next Move and Four Ways to Practice from native layout/text, with no artwork inputs. The scene renderer rebuilds them automatically. The registry lists canonical face-bearing boards as `post_only`, linked to their variants with `covers`. These canonical boards stay on the page and replace the variants during video editing. Permission for Gemini to draw illustrated people is separate from this upload restriction.
