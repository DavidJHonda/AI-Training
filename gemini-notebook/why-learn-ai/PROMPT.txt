TEACH THE COMPLETE LESSON

Orient with Where AI Already Lives, then explain each function and every named example: Recommends, Navigation, Face Recognition, Voice Assistants, Chatbots. Land the pre-chatbot takeaway. Bridge from familiar use to deliberate learning: start now, learn strengths and struggles, practice on something meaningful, and carry experience forward. Do not skip those instructions to reach graphic design. Explain drafting-table work, desktop publishing on a teenager’s desk, and posters, magazines, and brochures made while developing skill. Preserve the skill/tool distinction and explicitly apply it to AI. Introduce Why You’ll Thrive briefly, then develop This Is Your Time, You’ll Move Faster, and Build Good Habits Early with every instruction, without repeating the overview. Put this opportunity in historical perspective: steam engine/physical labor, electricity/factories, internet/information; AI could reach broadly. Do not claim earlier inventions only handled narrow tasks or promise AI outcomes. Attribute the quotation to the July 2025 White House strategy and name its title. Close by returning to the press metaphor. Do not narrate the activity.
