## What a reroll would buy

The teaching is there in all eight. What the new method would change is voice. Every one of these rolls narrates the lesson in inflated register: "securing your long-term competitive edge," "behavioral regimen," "strict boundary of authority," "dual action methodology," "operational blind spots," "hyper-physical." The page reads like a teacher talking to one student; the videos read like a consultant. A verbatim-script prompt (the brain-break pattern) or a "read the prose as written" rule would pull the narration back to the page's voice. That is a quality call for David, not a teaching failure.

Text-dense lessons (Next Level Moves 930 page words, Make Your Move 648, Honesty 621) produce 4:30–4:47 videos. Complete beats concise, and cuts can come later.
