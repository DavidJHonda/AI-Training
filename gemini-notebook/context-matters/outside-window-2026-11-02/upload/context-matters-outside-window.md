### Files, Websites, and Other Apps

Having your assignment open on your laptop doesn’t mean AI can use it.

Share the relevant material in the chat, or use the app’s tools to make it available.

### Earlier Conversations

And don’t assume AI has everything you said in another chat.

Memory and projects can bring information forward, but you may still need to supply missing details.

Before you ask, check: does AI have the information it needs?
