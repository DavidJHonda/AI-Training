# What Is AI?

Ask your desk at school, “Give me 10 ideas for my next history project.”

Nothing. It’s a desk.

![Ask the Desk. Ask AI. Luke asks a school desk for history-project ideas; Nate asks AI and receives a list. AI is software built to do things that used to take a human brain.](what-is-ai-ask-the-desk.jpg)

Ask a friend, and they can help you brainstorm. Ask AI, and it can give you a list in seconds.

**AI is software built to do things that used to take a human brain.** Understand a question. Summarize information. Translate a conversation. Suggest ideas for a history project.

That doesn’t mean it thinks like your friend, or that all 10 ideas will be good. But you now have a tool that can help with tasks that once needed a person.

Different AI systems do different jobs. Let’s look at two kinds you already use.

![Two Ways You Already Use AI. Recommendation AI chooses from existing options; generative AI uses learned patterns to create a new output from your prompt, meaning the question or instructions you give it. AI can recommend. AI can create. This course focuses on generative AI.](what-is-ai-1-types.jpg)

Here’s what that difference looks like.

![One Picks. One Creates. Recommendation AI selects an existing superhero movie. Generative AI writes a new superhero scene from a request.](what-is-ai-2-same-goal.jpg)

Two kinds. One picks, one creates.

This course is about the one that creates.
