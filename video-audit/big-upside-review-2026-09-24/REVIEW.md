## Materials notes for any next roll

1. **Card names are an instruction, not a requirement.** "Teach all six cards, three per
   board, by name" — both rolls ignored it. If the names matter, list them in REQUIRED
   VERBATIM AUDIO, for example "New antibiotics. Researchers screened thousands of compounds
   and found abaucin, which attacks a resistant bacterium."
2. **The quotation is not a required line.** Roll 1 turned it into reported speech; the live
   roll dropped it. Add it with its speaker.
3. **"About" and "over" keep getting dropped** ("a minute", "in 190 countries"). Say so in the
   prompt, as the Big Downside notes also recommend.
4. **No ban on board pointers beyond "this board shows."** Roll 1 says "Look at the function
   box", "the bottom comparison", "the banner at the bottom". Same fix as Big Downside
   materials bug 1.
5. **Notebook draws invented numbers** (10^80 "configurations", 1,420 states, 100x speed,
   200,000,000+). The prompt already bans invented facts; these came from drawings, not
   narration, so they are an edit-time cost rather than a prompt fix.
