🗣️ NARRATIVE ARC:
Last lesson a token moved through the layers, its numbers changing at every step. AI could try to look the finished numbers up in its table of token embeddings, but they are one of a kind, lining up with no token in the table. So how does AI work out what the token means when its vector matches nothing on file? It finds the closest match. That sounds simple, but there's more to it. A regular map finds any place from two numbers: Dallas is 33 north, 97 west. New York City and Mountain View track the same two dimensions with different values. Turn it around: given 38 north and 120 west you can still name the closest city, Mountain View; given 39 north and 70 west, New York City. Neither pair is a city. They are positions matching nothing on file, and you found the closest one anyway. AI does this with meaning instead of geography. In the taste test Coke and Pepsi match on six dimensions and split on Citrus, so their rows sit much closer to each other than either sits to Coffee. Drawn as a map, they sit side by side in the soft drinks neighborhood while Coffee is across in the hot drinks neighborhood. Same question: if the numbers were 9, 1, 10, 2, 3, 8, 9, which drink is closest? A fizz of 10 puts it with the sodas, a citrus of 9 puts it beside Pepsi. It matches nothing on file exactly, and it does not have to. That move has a name: add up the gap between two sets of numbers across every dimension and you get their distance. That is how AI measures closeness. AI does the same at scale, every token carrying thousands of dimensions, not seven, learned during training. That closeness is no accident: training nudged every token's numbers until words used in similar ways ended up in similar places, so landing near a token is how the meaning gets read. AI cannot look the one-of-a-kind numbers up, but it can read the token's position on a map where similar meanings sit close together. That is vector space. Back to our sentence: on its own, IT could have pointed at almost anything. Through the layers its numbers kept moving toward CAT. By the end they matched no token exactly, but of every meaning AI knows they sat closest to CAT. AI has figured out that IT means the cat.
