## NAVIGATE THE FUTURE

# Work Changes

Before you know it, you’ll be finished with school and starting your career. There’s lots of talk these days about how AI is changing jobs. What might that mean for you?

AI can change a job by changing the tasks within it. An entrepreneur, teacher, or lawyer might keep the same job title while doing very different work each day.

Keep two ideas in mind. First, you already know AI is especially useful for certain kinds of tasks. Those are good places to start using it at work. Second, what will set you apart is what you already know.

Now put those ideas together on your first assignment at work, and see how the work changes with AI.

### Board 1: Your First Assignment

**Image file:** `../course-assets/work-changes/work-changes-assignment-short.jpg`

![Your First Assignment](../course-assets/work-changes/work-changes-assignment-short.jpg)

**Teaching content:**

Your first assignment.

Your manager tells you, “We’re getting bad customer feedback on our new product. Read the last 500 reviews and tell us what’s happening and how we should fix it.”

**Stage 1: Routine Work.**

Learn what customers are saying.

**Before AI:** You read all 500 reviews, group the complaints, build the spreadsheet, and write a summary.

**With AI:** AI organizes the reviews and drafts a summary. You spot-check the results and investigate anything that looks wrong.

**Stage 2: Judgment & Decisions.**

Decide what to do about it.

**Before AI:** You investigate the causes, compare fixes, and present your recommendation.

**With AI:** AI helps you explore causes and compare fixes. You evaluate its suggestions and present your recommendation.

**Same assignment. Same responsibility. A different way to do the work.**

Before AI, you did all of the work yourself. With AI’s help, the grunt work gets done faster, giving you more time for higher-value work: understanding what the feedback means and deciding what to do. The more you know, the better you’ll be at that work.

## The Concepts

When people talk about how AI is changing jobs, you’ll hear two terms: automate and augment.

### Board 2: Two Ways AI Changes Work

**Image file:** `work-changes-automation-and-augmentation.jpg`

![Two Ways AI Changes Work](work-changes-automation-and-augmentation.jpg)

**Teaching content:**

Two ways AI changes work.

Automate. AI takes over a step. It sorted the reviews, grouped the ideas, and created the first summary.

Augment. AI helps a person do more. AI helped you explore more explanations, compare more options, and improve your recommendation.

The work still has your name on it. You own the outcome.

## A New Problem

AI also creates a new challenge for people starting their careers. In many professions, the grunt work has been a valuable training ground.

In our example, reading the reviews helps you learn how customers think, what they care about, and how they describe their experiences with the product. If AI does most of that reading for you, how will you build that understanding?

Here’s the risk: **AI can make you productive before it makes you knowledgeable.**

You might be asked to investigate a problem and recommend what to do before you understand the field well enough to judge AI’s suggestions.

What can you do? Use some of the time AI saves to build your own understanding. In our example, read a sample of the reviews and write down what you notice before looking at AI’s summary. Then compare: what did you miss, and what did AI miss?

What can you do today? Learn. And learn more. And you’re in the best place in the world to do it: school.

## Lab: Turn Reviews into Recommendations

Now try the assignment yourself using [100 fictional reviews of this course](../packets/course-reviews.txt). Use AI to find patterns, investigate the complaints, and recommend what to improve.

### Open the Reviews

Select Open Reviews. Read the first five reviews and write down what you notice. Keep that note so you can compare your observations with AI’s summary later. Now imagine reading and organizing the other 95 before you’re ready to make a recommendation.

### Compare with Your Notes

After AI’s first response, compare AI’s summary with what you noticed in the first five reviews. What matches? What’s different? Remember, AI saw all 100 reviews. Check the original reviews to see whether a difference reflects a broader pattern or something AI missed.

### Investigate Why

Look for patterns in the lowest-rated reviews. Ask AI what they have in common, then read the 2-star reviews yourself. Does the evidence support AI’s explanation? What else would you need to know before recommending a fix?

**Prompt:** What patterns do you see in the lowest-rated reviews? Give review IDs that support each pattern, and point out any exceptions.

### Catch What the Summary Missed

Summaries can leave out less common complaints. Ask AI to find them, then locate those complaints in the original reviews. Which deserve attention, even if only a few customers mention them?

**Prompt:** What are the strangest or rarest complaints in here, the ones a summary would miss?

### Prep the Presentation

Ask ChatGPT for two or three possible recommendations. Choose one yourself and explain why the reviews support it. Then have ChatGPT prepare the presentation around your choice. The deck is AI’s work. The decision inside it is yours. Review the slides before presenting them. Check that the evidence matches the reviews and the recommendation matches your decision.

### Record Your Decision

After choosing your recommendation, write these yourself, in your own words. One sentence each is enough. Point to the reviews you checked and explain your choice.

- I checked ___ against review(s) ___.
- I kept, changed, or rejected ___ because ___.
- I chose ___ because ___.

Your notes save in this browser. Copy them into your presentation if you want to keep or share them.
