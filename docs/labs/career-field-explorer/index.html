Build Your Skills · Project Labs

One app. Eight visible improvements.

Students build a Career Field Explorer across the section. Each lab adds one capability, tests one durable AI skill, and leaves a recovery file behind.

The governing rule: the app organizes evidence and helps a student choose what to investigate next. It never ranks careers, calculates a match, names a winner, or makes the decision.
