# Where Will AI Land?

In 1902, Hanoi had a problem. There were too many rats in the city’s new sewers. No worries. They devised a perfect plan. Turn in a rat’s tail, collect a payment. Tails poured in by the thousands. The brilliance of the plan!

Then people saw something very strange: rats running around the city with no tails.

Here is what happened. Rats can live a relatively full “rat life” even without their tails, and keep having more rat babies. The rat hunters, even without AI, came up with a clever plan to keep the rat-tail supply strong. They clipped the tails, but let the rats live.

A dead rat pays once. A live rat pays forever.

Some rat entrepreneurs went even further. They started rat farms. You probably know where this is going. When Hanoi stopped the program, they had a mountain of rat tails, but more rats than when it started.

**Unexpected Result**

They barely controlled the pests, poisoned native animals, and spread across the country.

Wider Highways. Houston spent $2.8 billion widening the Katy Freeway to ease congestion.

**Unexpected Result**

Rush-hour trips grew 30% to 55% longer between 2011 and 2014. More road space encouraged more driving.

## The Future of AI

These stories show how different the results can be from what people expected. The predictions we hear from AI Optimists and AI Worriers could be wrong, too. Nobody knows exactly where AI will land. The results could be very different from what people expect.

The most important thing AI does in your lifetime, good or bad, may be something nobody in any camp, any lab, or any course has thought of yet.

That uncertainty gives us a reason to keep paying attention.

## Closing Message

Some of the biggest results are the ones nobody predicted.

That’s the best reason to stay curious.
