## Arc position

**Before:** Be Curious (`becurious`). Short, habit-shaped, wide on purpose: reps are free, try the
new thing the week it ships, keep a list of what surprised you. Its close board sticky already
hands off: "The questions you chase become your edge."

**After:** What You Learned (`whatyoulearned`), the recap plus the future final. That neighbor is
load-bearing for this design: the lesson that follows this one is *already* the course recap, so
Make Your Move should not be recap-shaped. See Structure for what that costs the five calls.

**Opening bridge intent:** turn from curiosity into commitment. Curiosity is wide, and wide has a
limit: nobody gets picked for being generally interested. The bridge names that limit and pivots to
narrowing, which is the whole lesson. Transition sits at the top of this lesson, per house rule; no
lesson titles are named.

It earns the capstone slot: the lesson argues that depth is what catches what AI misses, and the
activity makes the student do exactly that, five times, before the course stops teaching.

## Judgment calls for David

1. **Five calls tighten to three.** Recommended. Three of buildedge's five calls were cross-
   reference recaps, sitting directly before the course's recap lesson, and they pushed the
   directive material to position 3. The trim folds "what do I use AI for" into the depth call and
   "how do I check my work" into the hand-off call, keeping both citations (including the required
   fix) but not their headings. Reversing this is cheap: restore the two numbered headings and put
   the numbers back to five in three places.

2. **Thesis echo with the rebuilt Skills That Matter.** That lesson opens two slots earlier in this
   same section on "production got cheap, judgment got valuable"; this lesson opens on the same
   claim before turning to the squeeze. The audit flagged the pair and kept both when they were
   headed to different sections. They are no longer far apart. My compression cuts the overlap to
   roughly one sentence. If you want zero echo, the clean split is: Skills That Matter owns
   production-versus-direction, and this lesson opens on the squeeze paragraph cold.

3. **"Make things, not just prompts" leaves the box; "Pick one real interest" is deleted.** The
   HOW TO BUILD DEPTH box goes from five cards to three. Recommended: the promoted line is one of
   your two named directives and was buried mid-grid, and the deleted card restated the "Pick one,
   not five." kicker sitting directly above it. Cost: a three-card box is thinner than the
   five-card version you approved originally.

4. **The sparring-partner citation is name-free.** Drafted as "the one you practiced earlier in
   this section" rather than naming AI Tips, since AI Tips is still a working title and this would
   be the only place in the course that hard-codes it. The by-name variant, if you prefer the
   anchor: "Use the second mode too, the one **AI Tips** gave you: AI as sparring partner."
