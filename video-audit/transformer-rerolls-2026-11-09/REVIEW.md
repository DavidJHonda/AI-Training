## Technical basis for accuracy flags

The original paper treats embeddings as learned vectors, not dictionary definitions.
It supplies positional information in the input representation; parallel computation
does not itself destroy an existing word sequence. Its decoder also restricts a
position's access to subsequent positions. These support the flagged statements and
visuals without adding technical machinery to the student lesson.
[Attention Is All You Need, sections 3.1, 3.4, and 3.5](https://arxiv.org/html/1706.03762v7).
