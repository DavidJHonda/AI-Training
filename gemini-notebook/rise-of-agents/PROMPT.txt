TEACH THE COMPLETE LESSON
Open on the question and the analogy board: GPS is like ChatGPT, self-driving is like an agent, each with its full explanation, including that you may not catch a mistake until later. Say agents are everywhere because they do the work. Then the comparison board: the scenario with 30 points, Friday’s game, a friend’s phone video, and 50 clips; everything you do with a chatbot and the one thing AI does; then everything the agent does and what you still own. Explain that an agent runs on the same kind of LLM and that what changes is what happens after you type. Teach all four loop steps by name with their descriptions, the go-again return to the plan, and the banner line. Give the responsibility point in full. Then rogue agents: April 2026, PocketOS, the permissions error, the master key, the live database and backups deleted in nine seconds, and the agent’s quotation; then 2025, Gemini, the wiped project files, the apology, and its quotation. Finish on the never-without-review rule and the rule of thumb, including starting with ChatGPT. Do not narrate the on-page activity, promise an agent always finishes, or suggest an agent should work around a block, a limit, or a permission.
