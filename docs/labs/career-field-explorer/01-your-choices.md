# Lab 1: Build the first useful version

**Lesson:** Your Choices  
**Time:** 12–15 minutes  
**Starter:** Build from the prompt in a new project chat  
**Recovery:** `01-your-choices-recovery.html`

## Build

Build a Career Field Explorer with six consistent career-field cards. The page
should explain that students are exploring, not choosing a career today. Use
meaningful example content, not placeholder text.

## Prompt to use with AI

> Build the first version of a Career Field Explorer. Add six responsive career-field
> cards: Health and Patient Care, Skilled Trades and Technical Work, Technology and
> Digital Systems, Teaching and Education, Law and Legal Services, and Business and
> Operations. Give every card three clearly labeled parts: Why Explore, What You’d Do,
> and What to Know. Keep each part concise and use meaningful information instead of
> placeholder text. Make it clear that the tool helps students explore career fields
> without choosing one for them.

## Test before finishing

- The file opens directly in a browser.
- Six field cards appear and remain readable on a phone-width screen.
- Every card has real example content.
- Every card clearly labels Why Explore, What You’d Do, and What to Know.
- The page never claims to know which career is best for the student.

## What this lab teaches

The model or reasoning control may change how AI builds the file, but the student is
responsible for defining the job and checking the result.
