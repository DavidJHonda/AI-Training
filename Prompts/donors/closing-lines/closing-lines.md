# Closing Lines — "Be Smarter Than the Tool"

Ten paired closing statements from the course. Each pair is one beat: the
statement, then its takeaway.

1. AI is today's printing press. — Learn to run it.
2. The tool answers. You evaluate. — Read. Understand. Validate.
3. It learned patterns, not facts. — Fluency comes free. Being right doesn't.
4. The math is simple. — It's conditional probability, run countless times.
5. Computers don't read text. — Tokens convert language into readable numbers.
6. Meaning builds up, layer by layer. — Attention and transformation. Dozens of times.
7. Meaning is a position. — Close in space is close in meaning.
8. AI learned from what we wrote. — Flaws in the data become flaws in the answers.
9. Be the one to quit when it's time. — AI won't quit for you.
10. Seeing or hearing isn't proof anymore. — Check the source, not the pixels.
