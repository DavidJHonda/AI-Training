PRIORITIZED FINDINGS

1. Correct the privacy status labels in the percentage example (1:41.8-2:03.47).
The useful core is the contrast between an ambiguous percentage and the relevant
context (free throws versus physics). However, the graphic moves from "Privacy:
Secured" to "Privacy: Exposed!" and "Sensitive Personal Record Sent" merely as
physics context is identified. At 1:58 the image shows "Physics Grade" without
an actual personal grade or identifying record. At 2:00 it shows the study topic
"Physics: Rotation and Dynamics" while retaining the exposed label. This teaches
an unnecessary binary: vague prompts are secure, useful context is a disclosure.
The lesson instead asks for enough relevant context while limiting sensitive data.
Proposed narrow repair: preserve the phone/assistant and comparison; replace the
status language with "Needs context", "Useful context", and "Share only what is
needed" as appropriate. Remove the unsupported personal-record warning. Inspect
all animated states before choosing overlay boundaries. Keep audio unchanged.

2. Remove the fixed retention/deletion promise (3:28.60-3:37.40).
At 3:33 the diagram explicitly says "Deletion Policy Window (30-Day Retention)".
At 3:36 it reaches "Day 30 / 30 (Threshold Met)", "Record Purged from Storage",
and "PERMANENTLY DELETED". Neither the page nor the narration supplies that policy;
both say retention depends on the app. No specific service or hypothetical example
is identified. The visual converts a qualified explanation into an apparent
universal promise. This finding is about unsupported specificity, not a claim that
no provider ever has a 30-day policy.
Proposed narrow repair: retain the useful device-versus-company comparison, replace
the countdown and guaranteed purge state with "A copy may remain" and "Retention
and deletion depend on the service". Simplify technical labels if rebuilding this
scene. Keep audio unchanged. Both the initial and final state need correction.
