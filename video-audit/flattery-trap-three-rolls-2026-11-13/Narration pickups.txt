Record the passages below in the lesson voice. Headings in brackets are editing cues. Read everything else exactly.

[Definition]
The Flattery Trap is treating AI’s approval as honest evaluation.

[Gatsby — first reply, then useful feedback]
Great start! You’ve clearly identified the central theme. This is a strong foundation.

You’ve named your topic: the American Dream. But ‘Fitzgerald explores this idea’ leaves me wondering what you think he shows about it. What does Gatsby’s experience suggest about that dream?

[Training — separate takes]
One way AI learns from this feedback is called reinforcement learning from human feedback, or RLHF.

Sometimes reviewers choose the answer that offers praise, even when the criticism would be more useful.

The praise literally gets baked into the system.

[After the quote]
OpenAI now tests ChatGPT for sycophancy. But you’ll still see it.

[Introduce the three moves]
Here’s how to get better feedback.

[Closing board — read both lines in order]
AI can learn to tell you what you want to hear.
Steer it toward feedback you can use.
