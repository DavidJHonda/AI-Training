## Scene and question review

### 1. Mind Reading

AI reads your mind through your cell phone. That’s how its answers sound so personal.

Visual: A sketched phone sends colorful signal waves toward a cartoon thought bubble.

Correction after answering: AI uses your message and the context available to it to generate a response. It cannot read your mind.

### 2. Tokens

AI tokens are arcade coins. Every time you ask a question, a tiny robot drops one into a machine.

Visual: A tiny sketched robot drops a coin into an arcade machine.

Correction after answering: In this course, tokens are pieces of text that AI processes as numbers. No arcade coins required.

### 3. Training

Training an AI means making it run laps around a data center. The more laps it runs, the smarter it gets.

Visual: A computer wearing running shoes circles a row of server cabinets.

Correction after answering: During training, AI adjusts its parameters using data. It learns through numerical updates, not exercise.

### 4. Layers

AI adds layers when it gets cold. Bigger models need bigger jackets.

Visual: A computer is bundled in an oversized stack of colorful jackets.

Correction after answering: Layers are processing stages that update the numbers inside the model. They are not clothing.

### 5. Attention

Attention is how AI stays awake late at night, even after its bedtime.

Visual: A wide-eyed computer sits beside a crescent moon and an alarm clock.

Correction after answering: Attention weighs information from related tokens. It has nothing to do with sleep.

### 6. Claude-A-Roni

ChatGPT and Gemini run on computer chips, but Claude runs on a special type of pasta called Claude-A-Roni.

Visual: ChatGPT and Gemini labels sit above computer chips; Claude sits above a bowl of rotini with a Claude-A-Roni label.

Correction after answering: Claude runs on computers too. The pasta-powered model is a joke.

### 7. Closing

Now you know how AI really works. Please do not put pasta in your computer.
