# Unexpected Results: SMS wording

The two retained examples are Text Messaging and Cane Toads. The texting example
now emphasizes unexpectedly widespread adoption, especially among young people.

Primary evidence checked on October 23, 2026:

- [Interview with SMS co-inventor Friedhelm Hillebrand, Detecon](https://www.detecon.com/en/insights/interview/the-biggest-detecon-project-of-all-time-and-the-invention-of-sms):
  Hillebrand describes SMS as one of his planned GSM data services. Asked whether
  he expected its success, he says it was unexpected. He attributes adoption in
  part to young people and its lower cost than calling.
- [Vodafone's account of the first text message](https://www.vodafone.com/news/newsroom/technology/25-anniversary-text-message):
  Neil Papworth says he did not anticipate how popular texting would become.

These sources support surprise about adoption. They do not establish that SMS
was originally designed specifically for carriers to communicate with customers.
That narrower origin claim is therefore omitted rather than treated as verified.
