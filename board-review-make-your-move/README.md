# Make Your Move Career Board Review

These two approved boards replace the six-row career table with two successive
Editorial Explainer: Three-Card Full-Bleed boards. The exact reviewed JPGs are now
used by both the on-page lesson and the video-prep materials.

Each profession uses a new Nate-and-Luke teaching scene. The artwork shows AI
handling support tasks while people retain judgment, relationships, physical
action, leadership, and responsibility. Each card uses the current 56 px board
title, 40 px card title, 29 px body, 20 px section-label, 16:9 artwork, locked
accent, 10% wash, and 22% border requirements.

## Artwork generation

The six source scenes in `assets/` were created with the built-in image generator
using `course-assets/work-changes/work-changes.jpg` as the identity and photographic-style
reference. Every prompt asked for a 16:9 future-career scene in which an AI screen
handles the listed support tasks while Nate and Luke visibly perform the profession’s
human-owned work. The role-specific prompts covered medical care, classroom
adaptation, client legal strategy, on-site electrical diagnosis, creative direction,
and customer-facing entrepreneurship.
