# Destination map for the shorter core course

**Draft for discussion · October 12, 2026 · All 59 current routed pages**

The [proposed syllabus](core-course-proposal-2026-10-12.md) has 15 required lessons. This map shows where each current page contributes and what happens to its practice. It follows the current navigation order and displayed labels in `SECTION_GROUPS` and `SECTION_META` in [index.html](../index.html).

**Core** means reuse or adapt the named material for the numbered proposed lesson. It does not mean copying the entire original page. **Go deeper** means optional material linked from the relevant core lesson. **Extension** means a coherent longer sequence. **Utility** means access without a core completion requirement. Every original page remains available in the full course; no deletion or live change is proposed by this map.

Where a lesson contributes a brief excerpt to the core and also belongs to an extension, both are stated. Activities do not automatically follow the lesson text into the core. Historical drafts and donor files that are absent from the live route are not additional lessons in this inventory.

## Coverage and decision notes

This inventory contains 8 Start Smarter pages, 9 Work With AI pages, 10 Understand AI pages, 9 Avoid Traps pages, 9 Embrace the Future pages, 9 Build Your Skills pages, and 5 Finish Smarter pages. The 59 pages include six section openers, Welcome, The Final, and three utilities; they are not 59 teaching lessons of equal length. The live route currently marks For Groups optional. The proposed core also removes reviews and resources from its required progress count.

The core retains concrete responses to invented claims, incomplete documents, misleading agreement, misplaced interpersonal trust, and convincing fakes. Training bias, engagement, ethics, and agent permissions contribute bounded essentials where students need them, with their fuller treatment available afterward. This is a deliberately narrower literacy course; it does not claim the full curriculum’s technical, societal, or career breadth.

The largest editorial work is in core 1, 9, 11, 13, and the adapted practices in 4, 6, and 14. Most other core lessons keep an existing teaching job while reducing examples or practice length. Test these combinations before changing the full course’s assets or investing in new videos.
