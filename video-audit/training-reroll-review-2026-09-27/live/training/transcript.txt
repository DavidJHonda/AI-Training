[0:12.00-0:18.00] You take a shot, check where the ball goes, and adjust your aim or how much physical force you use on the next try.
[0:18.00-0:23.00] AI training follows a similar pattern – guess, check, and adjust.
[0:23.00-0:29.00] Instead of physical force, the training process modifies the internal numbers that determine the model's output.

[0:50.00-0:52.00] Step three is the adjust.
[0:52.00-0:58.00] The system modifies the model's internal numbers to make the word jelly more likely in this exact situation.
[0:58.00-1:01.00] The loop runs again on the next example.
