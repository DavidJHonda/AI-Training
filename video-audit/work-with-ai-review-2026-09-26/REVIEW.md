# Work With AI — video review

Reviewed September 26, 2026. Nine current lesson videos; LABs and TRY IT activities excluded.

**Recommendation: repair selectively.** The biggest section-wide weakness is sustained board time. AI Is Different needs the broadest narration work. Your Home Base, Questions Matter, and Context Window retain their core teaching. The Art of Prompting needs only small cleanup. Several missing or awkward spoken lines are sensible Fola candidates, but no replacement audio has yet been generated or auditioned.

This is a completed page/transcript comparison, board-duration measurement, and sampled-frame review. **It is not final audiovisual sign-off:** I did not hear the audio or watch every frame end to end. All narration quotes are from ASR. Voice quality, seams, pronunciation, continuous animation, and the suspected cut in Critical Thinking remain listening/viewing checks.

## Priorities

1. **Evaluate the Results — repair the viewing rhythm.** From **0:42.5 to 4:06**, five teaching boards run continuously for **203.5 seconds**. Including the closing card, it is **214.4 seconds to the end**. The useful decision process becomes a long narrated handout. Restore the student-facing labels and the simple “You decide whether the answer holds up” line, and identify relevant drawing footage before rebuilding.
2. **AI Is Different — repair narration before polishing visuals.** It teaches the password, robot/chef, PS5 and messy-input examples well. But it misses the Superman/Kryptonite comparison, input/output choices and the app-dependent qualification, and the training side of safety. It introduces its superpowers/Kryptonite framing only in the close. These are multiple missing connections; a new narration pass is more sensible than many tiny grafts.
3. **Where AI Works Best — keep the useful examples; break the 150-second board run.** All four strengths are explained, but their four required takeaway lines are not spoken. Add those lines and remove the unsupported “millions of examples” addition. Fola can help the narration; it cannot by itself supply the missing visual variety.
4. **Critical Thinking — resolve the possible cut at 2:37.** ASR stops at “alternative ex...” before habit four. Listen before declaring an audible defect. If clipped, one complete Fola sentence is preferable to a syllable splice. Preserve the strong chocolate-study story.
5. **Your Home Base, Context Window, Questions Matter — retain the teaching and improve the longest holds.** Their longest single boards last 68.5, 58, and 56 seconds respectively. The later board/drawing alternation in Questions Matter is a good pattern to preserve.
6. **Opener and Art of Prompting — focused cleanup.** Restore the opener’s memorable course language. The Art of Prompting already has good examples and visual alternation; two complete-sentence cuts remove repetitive formal phrasing.
