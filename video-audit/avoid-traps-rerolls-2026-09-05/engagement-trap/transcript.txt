[1:57.50-2:04.50] To see why an AI is built to behave this way, we have to trace the behavior back through the history of web interface design.
[2:04.50-2:07.50] Early websites operated with built-in friction.

[2:21.50-2:28.50] But for the person holding the mouse, that tiny delay served as a crucial checkpoint to pause and evaluate their own intentions.

[2:32.50-2:38.50] Content lived on discrete pages with hard, unavoidable endings, reaching the bottom forced to pause.
[2:38.50-2:43.50] It required you to make an active, conscious choice about whether you wanted to consume more.

[2:49.50-2:51.50] The mechanics were simple.
[2:51.50-2:55.50] As you approach the bottom of the screen, new content automatically populates.
[2:55.50-2:59.50] The page break vanishes and continuing becomes the default action.
[2:59.50-3:05.50] By eliminating the friction of that physical click, the interface also eliminated the moment of choice.
[3:06.50-3:14.50] The behavioral loop this created was so potent that Raskan himself later publicly expressed regret over its addictive impact on users.
[3:14.50-3:21.50] When an interface removes the natural stopping points, endless consumption becomes the default human reaction.
[3:21.50-3:26.50] Today, modern AI chat interfaces have engineered their own version of that missing page break.
[3:26.50-3:31.50] When a chatbot immediately fires off follow-up questions after answering your prompt,
[3:31.50-3:35.50] it functions mechanically just like a bottomless feed or an auto-playing video.
[3:35.50-3:40.50] The technology platforms hosting these tools rely on extended user engagement metrics.
[3:40.50-3:46.50] They benefit when you spend more time on the screen, maintain usage streaks, and build daily habits.
[3:46.50-3:50.50] The AI suggestions are not inherently malicious.
[3:50.50-3:56.50] If you genuinely need a deep dive into algebra, accepting those practice questions is a perfectly good use of your time.
[3:56.50-4:00.50] The risk surfaces because your initial goal of getting a quick formula

[4:18.50-4:22.50] Ask yourself if continuing the conversation serves your immediate objective
[4:22.50-4:25.50] or if it just feeds the platform's engagement metrics.
