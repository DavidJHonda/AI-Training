**Retain the teaching and the existing edit. Consider a targeted visual pacing
pass, with Two Ways AI Changes the Work as the first priority.** There is no
identified reason for a wholesale reroll. Final narration approval remains
subject to listening.

between AI doing a step and helping someone do more, and the closing problem of
being productive before having the knowledge to check the work. The sequence
connects prior knowledge → changed daily work → responsibility → continued learning.
