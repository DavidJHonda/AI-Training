## Changes from the approved plan, and why

- **The Reshape examples stay on roll 3's drawing through "rewrite technical instructions."** The plan returned to the board for that line. Roll 3 drew a technical-instructions card for it, so Edit Spec 8b prefers the drawing. The board returns for the takeaway.
- **Rings on a board's return start at the return frame.** The takeaway ring (or the whole-illustration ring on Board 1) appears when the board comes back, 0.2-0.3 s before the spoken onset. Otherwise the previous section's ring would flash for a fraction of a second on the cut back.
- **No whole-card ring at each strength's name.** The name is spoken within 2 s of the board arriving, so the full view opens unmarked (Edit Spec 3) and the first ring is WHY. The approved v6 treatment was the same.
- **Board 1 ring timing.** The A+ ring starts at "AI successfully wrote the code" rather than "earning an A+", and the C− ring at "on the right side" rather than "scored a C-minus". The plan's onsets would have held each ring for under 1.5 s before the cutaway.
- **The G4 inner gap was not tightened.** Measured by RMS, the real gap between "More possibilities." and "You choose the direction." is about 0.7 s, not 1.44 s.
- **Pause before the close: +12 frames of matched room tone (0.62 → 1.02 s).** The plan expected about 1.0 s there, but the join measured 0.62 s.
- **The phrase cut was executed:** roll 3 95.50-96.10 was removed. whisper's word times put "from" at 95.20-95.46 and "millions" at 95.46. The first attempt (95.33-96.10) cut into "from" and read "patterns **of** ideas". Six candidate cut points were then transcribed: every one from 2864 to 2866 → 2882 to 2884 reads "patterns from ideas and examples". I chose 2865 → 2883, the quietest trough (42 dB). This join is below the whole-beat rule, and **only an ear test certifies it**.
