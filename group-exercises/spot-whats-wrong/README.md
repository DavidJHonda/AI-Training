# Spot What’s Wrong: image review

15 AI-generated draft images for a follow-up to Be the Last Person Standing. Created with the built-in image-generation tool. These are not yet connected to a course activity.

Open `review.html` to inspect the set. Each image has a collapsible answer. The three difficulty groups are provisional and should be tried with students on a classroom screen before final ordering.

All pictures were deliberately prompted to include an inconsistency. This is an observation exercise, not a reliable method for detecting AI. An odd detail can also result from editing, staging, or an unusual real object. Finding nothing wrong does not establish that an image is real.

## Answer key

1. **Porch Dog (Easy)**: The dog has six legs. Count the six separate paws attached to one body.
2. **School Bicycle (Easy)**: The bicycle’s front wheel has no fork connecting it to the frame.
3. **Breakfast Fork (Easy)**: The fork has one head but branches into two handles.
4. **Basketball Hoop (Easy)**: The basketball intersects the solid, unbroken backboard.
5. **Cat Mirror (Easy)**: The cat’s mouth is closed, but its reflection is yawning.
6. **Cafe Lemonade (Medium)**: The straw passes through the side of the glass below the rim.
7. **Backpack (Medium)**: The backpack strap passes through the student’s upper arm.
8. **Birthday Cake (Medium)**: The front-right candle has a flame pointing downward.
9. **Skateboard (Medium)**: One skateboard wheel is mounted on top of the deck.
10. **Front Porch (Medium)**: The porch railing blocks the top of the stairs, with no opening.
11. **Guitar (Harder)**: The guitar strings have a gap between the sound hole and the bridge.
12. **School Lockers (Harder)**: The open locker’s hinges are on its free edge, separated from the frame’s hinges.
13. **Picnic Basket (Harder)**: The basket handle passes through the middle of the plate.
14. **Shop Window (Harder)**: The reflection holds a yellow shopping bag, but the person’s hands are empty.
15. **Parked Bicycles (Harder)**: The two bicycle frames share a single central wheel.

## Review notes

- The dog generated six legs rather than the proposed five; the answer reflects the actual image.
- The café straw and guitar needed targeted revisions to make their clues visible.
- The bicycles share both front forks at one hub, rather than the originally proposed front/rear combination. The shared-wheel clue remains.
- The fork, blocked porch, skateboard, and unusual bicycle construction could be staged or custom-built in real life. Present them as deliberate oddities, not proof of AI.
- Strings and hinges are the smallest clues; validate them from the back of the room.
- Source paths, initial prompts, and revision prompts are recorded in `generation-prompts.json`. Only selected JPGs are kept here.
