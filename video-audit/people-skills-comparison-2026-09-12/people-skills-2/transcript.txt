[0:38.24-0:42.40] is wrong, and the people you rely on when things get difficult.

[1:19.60-1:23.56] We can structure this practice into four specific behaviors.
[1:23.56-1:26.08] Practice one is to listen to understand.

[1:42.28-1:44.84] Never assume you know what those cues mean.

[2:08.40-2:10.56] Separate the behavior from the individual.

[2:15.44-2:17.72] AI can suggest what to say.

[2:20.04-2:22.88] Learn someone's trust or do the rep for you.
