Illustrative values need not be sourced statistics. Effective Notebook emphasis may remain on supporting scenes. My earlier reasoning was too aggressive: absence from the lesson text, different skill labels, and fictional tool names did not establish that the scenes misled students. The updated rule makes this distinction explicit.
