# AI is Different: lines to speak

AI is taking the world by storm. It has superpowers that set it apart from any other software.

A human can hold Kryptonite, toss it in a backpack, whatever. Not Superman, for him it can be fatal.

AI has its own Kryptonite. It’s not fatal, but you need to be aware of it.

And no one, not the engineers who built it, the researchers who study it, or the company that ships it, can fully predict what it will do.

## Closing Message

AI’s foundation gives it new superpowers.

Those superpowers come with Kryptonite.
