TEACH THE COMPLETE LESSON
Start with what happens in the ten seconds after a clip hits your feed. On “Same Clip. Two Eras.” a friend forwards the principal’s school-closure announcement. The face, voice, and hallway look right. Then the student checks the school website and finds no current closure notice. Give each “Your call” in the student’s voice. Explain both ways to fall for the trap, including dismissing something true because it could be fake. Tell the Stanley Cup joke, where everyone is in on it, then go directly to “Why People Make Harmful Fakes.” Teach Money, Power, Fame, and Cruelty from left to right with each card’s reason. Name outrage, fear, excitement, and hope as cues to stop. Give the brief detector caution. On “Three Ways to Check a Clip.” read the questions for Source, Context, and Corroboration. Return to the principal’s clip. Find the full announcement, check its dates, and ask the school office using contact information you already have. Until the school confirms next week’s closure, it’s unverified. Speak the verification banner. Read “Fakes about you” in full, including what to write down, the trusted adult, the under-18 image instructions, platform reporting, Take It Down, and CyberTipline.
