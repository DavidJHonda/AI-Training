TEACH THE COMPLETE LESSON
Open with finishing school and the career question. Explain how tasks can change within a familiar job title, using entrepreneur, teacher, and lawyer. Connect AI’s useful tasks with what you already know. Introduce the assignment and read the manager’s words. Teach both stages: Routine Work, learning what customers say; Judgment & Decisions, deciding what to do. Within each, compare Before AI with With AI and cover every sentence. Explain organizing and summarizing, spot-checking, investigating, evaluating suggestions, and presenting your recommendation. Speak the same-assignment banner. Leave the board for the approved time-savings passage and the knowledge takeaway. Introduce automate and augment; explain each using the reviews example, then ownership. Turn to the learning problem: routine work has been a training ground. Reading reviews builds understanding of customers. Explain the productivity risk and making recommendations before knowing enough to judge AI’s suggestions. Answer with deliberate learning: read a sample, note observations before AI’s summary, then compare. Follow with what students can do today, school, and the closing lines. Do not restore retired strengths or productivity boards, promise a week becomes minutes, predict which jobs survive, add statistics, or narrate the lab.
