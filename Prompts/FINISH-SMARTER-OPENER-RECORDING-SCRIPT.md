# A Message from Luke and Nate

**Target length:** Approximately 90 seconds to two minutes  
**Delivery:** Warm, direct, and conversational. Speak to one student, not an audience.

## Recording script

**Luke:** Congratulations. You made it to the end of *Be Smarter Than the Tool*. We hope you enjoyed our course. Some subjects were tough, but we hope you still had some fun.

**Nate:** You now have a foundation for understanding AI. When someone makes a big claim about it, you’ll know what questions to ask.

*[Optional visual: briefly show a few moments from the course or one of Luke and Nate’s projects.]*

**Luke:** And here’s our prediction. Ten years from now, you’ll think back to this course and return to BeSmarterThanTheTool.com.

**Nate:** After shaking the cobwebs off the first page, you’ll re-read some of what you learned here. And you’ll chuckle to yourself, because your future AI knowledge will make this course feel like the training wheels on your first bike.

*[Optional visual: show the course home page during the ten-years-from-now prediction.]*

**Luke:** That’s what we want for every person our age. We hope this course is just the beginning of what you learn and build.

**Nate:** Before you go, take the Final and earn your certificate. Resources has ideas for what to do next. And tell us what you thought on Course Reviews. Your feedback helps us make the course better.

**Luke:** Thanks for learning with us.

**Nate:** And if you’re a hockey fan—

**Luke and Nate:** GO STARS!

*[Optional end card: the Five Big Ideas to Take With You poster, signed “Luke and Nate.”]*
