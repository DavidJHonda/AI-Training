What’s the magic that powers ChatGPT, Claude, and every other AI you’ve used? 
A big part of that math is probability: how likely something is. When AI builds an answer, it calculates probabilities for what comes next.
WHERE PROBABILITY MATH BEGAN
In 1654, two French mathematicians, Blaise Pascal and Pierre de Fermat, traded letters about gambling. Their work helped lay the foundation for modern probability.
Start with something simple: when every outcome is equally likely, you can calculate a probability by counting the possibilities.
The probability formula: ways to get the result divided by total possible outcomes equals probability.

CONDITIONAL PROBABILITY
New evidence can change the odds. Conditional probability takes that evidence into account.
A Clue Changes the Odds. You toss two coins. Someone peeks and tells you the first coin landed heads. What’s the chance that both coins landed heads now? Two outcomes are ruled out, leaving one way to get two heads out of two possible outcomes, or 50 percent.

Paste it in and ask AI
Not yours to share.
That conversation is someone else\u2019s private information, and it isn\u2019t yours to hand to a tool. Help them as a friend, or point them to someone who can. The AI doesn\u2019t need their secret to be useful to you.
Keep it between you two
Stop here.
Their private information stays private. If you want to help, do it as a friend. This is a moment to leave the tool out of it.
