# Build Your Skills opener: comparison of rolls 5, 6, and 7

November 4, 2026. Evaluation only; no candidate built or installed.

## Recommendation

**Roll 6 is the strongest overall fallback, but none earns KEEP under the current lesson and required wording. Recommend a more tightly scripted reroll before production.** Roll 6 teaches the complete section overview. Roll 7 develops the map most explicitly and gets through the bike analogy faster, but introduces stronger unsupported claims, narrates the screen, and talks beyond the closing. Roll 5 spends its detail on the analogy and omits essential material from the map.

The shared problem is script drift. All three replace plain language with formal expansions, and none speaks the map title “Build Your Skills.” None delivers the exact two-line close as two standalone sentences. All preserve “The skills are yours to keep,” but rewrite the first line; roll 7 adds another statement afterward. These are narration issues, not defects a replacement board can solve.

Do not assemble multiple narrators to recover individual words. The owner's reason for rerolling was voice continuity. No cross-roll audio graft is recommended or auditioned.

## Per-roll decisions

### Roll 5 — REROLL

Teaching and hard requirements are mapped above. The bike story is complete but overexpanded; the map has genuine missing content, not simply different wording. Honesty and privacy are absent, and creative thinking is only named in the map. The opening predicts that today's interface “will look completely different next year” (0:06–0:10); the source makes no such dated prediction. “Yours forever” (0:11–0:15), “permanently rewired” (1:17–1:23), and “zero competitive advantage” (1:55–2:02) add unwarranted certainty.

Source QA: PASS for the intended instructional arc. No lesson change is required to accommodate these expansions. Additions worth retaining: none essential; the inability to download a skill is understandable but redundant with “Nobody could give you balance.” Repair plan: not established. Rolls 6/7 supply the missing map meanings, but not the exact required wording, and voice-compatible joins are unheard. Editing notes: sampled frames show invented pseudo-text around 2:04 and an “Answer: Automated Output” label around 2:52 that is unhelpful for an open personal question; course-board substitutions and engine outro cleanup would also be required. Listening: not performed.

### Roll 6 — REROLL; strongest fallback

Teaching and hard requirements are mapped above. Its roadmap contains the essential content and its ending has no further spoken recap. It is the least problematic complete overview. However, the bike/practice passage occupies roughly 0:15–1:26 and repeatedly restates that the skill remains. Expressions such as “crucial invisible mechanism,” “permanent internal capability,” and “sense of equilibrium” are a poor fit for the source's direct voice. These are wording observations, not a judgment of vocal tone.

Errors/unsupported additions: “obsolete in a matter of months” (0:03–0:07) and the universal-access/“only variables” assertions (2:01–2:16). The latter was corroborated by medium ASR. Source QA: PASS; the page says choices/questions/judgment matter, without claiming they alone determine the quality of all work. Additions worth retaining: “feel the wobbles” (1:20–1:26) makes practice concrete, although it does not require a source edit. Repair plan: a shorter assembly could remove repetition and some overclaims, but would still miss the spoken map title and multiple required lines. No complete repair is verified. Editing notes: preserve useful bicycle, skating, and basketball illustrations; replace the invented download/protocol display at approximately 1:16–1:22 if this becomes the base. Listening: not performed.

### Roll 7 — REROLL

Teaching and hard requirements are mapped above. Best-developed map and the most economical bike story of the three. Those strengths do not outweigh its unsupported claims: “human abilities are permanent” (0:11), a “perfectly level” technological playing field and equal capabilities for everyone (1:23–1:32), personal choices/questions/judgment as “the only variables that dictate your success” (1:33–1:43), and a guarantee of retained skills (2:45–2:51). These passages were corroborated with medium ASR.

It also speaks production language: “the banner at the bottom” (2:43) and “Look at this final message on screen” (3:07). After the close it adds “True preparation relies entirely on developing your own mind, because your internal capability is the only asset that will never become obsolete” (3:15–3:22). That weakens the approved ending and adds another absolute claim. Source QA: PASS; these are additions, not errors required by the current source. Additions worth retaining: the explicit idea of selecting a concrete next step for a project is consistent with the labs, but needs no source change. Repair plan: several complete sentences could be cut, including the post-close addition, but deleting them does not restore every required line or the missing title. No complete verified repair. Listening: not performed.
