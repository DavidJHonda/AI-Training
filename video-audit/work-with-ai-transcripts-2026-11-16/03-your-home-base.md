# 3. Your Home Base

`course-assets/your-home-base/your-home-base.mp4` · 3:10 · 538 words

**[0:00]** When you sit down to use AI, your first decision is which app to open. Three names you'll hear constantly are ChatGPT from OpenAI, Claude from Anthropic, and Gemini from Google. All three can chat, write code, and answer questions. So if they all do essentially the same things, why does it matter which one you pick? Think about it like a burger place. In-N-Out and McDonald's both build cheeseburgers from roughly the same ingredients. Yet, they provide completely different experiences because each company operates on a different idea of what a restaurant should be. One focuses on a tiny menu with everything made fresh to order. The other is engineered for maximum speed, massive scale, and being exactly identical in every city on Earth.

**[0:46]** The big three AI apps work the same way. They all rely on AI models that learn patterns during training. But each company makes distinct choices about how to train those models, what to build into their app and how the system acts when it isn't sure. Those choices determine how each app feels, what specific tasks it excels at, and how it behaves. This board breaks down the big three side by side. We can think of ChatGPT as the anything box. It acts as one general purpose AI app designed for a wide range of tasks. You ask questions, create things, and get help with your daily work. This comes directly from OpenAI's driving question. how do we put capable AI in everyone's hands?

**[1:30]** Claude acts as the thinking partner. It functions specifically as an assistant for working through complex ideas and difficult multi-step tasks. Anthropic places a strong emphasis on safety and predictable behavior, asking, how do we build powerful AI we can actually trust?

**[1:47]** Gemini takes a different approach. It acts as the AI assistant built directly into Google. Its main advantage shows up when your work connects to the Google tools you already use on a daily basis. Google's guiding question focuses on integration. How do we put AI inside the tools people already use? Each app has distinct strengths born from these specific philosophies, but you will find massive overlap in what they can accomplish day to day. Because of this overlap, any of the three tools will do the job for most tasks. Do not agonize over the choice. There is no single best app, and what is best today might change by next week. Claude currently requires users to be at least 18 years old. We still cover it because knowing your options matters, even before you can access them. For this course, ChatGPT is your designated home base.

**[2:33]** Learn it deeply, its settings, its features, its quirks. Knowing one app thoroughly beats shallow dabbling in all three. Later, you can try a specific strategy. Ask a second app the exact same question. It might catch a detail the first app missed or suggest a completely different approach to the problem. If the two apps disagree, you have something to investigate. But keep in mind, even if they agree perfectly, that does not guarantee they are right.

**[2:59]** Pick a home base. Learn it deeply. The skills transfer. The app is just where you practice them.
