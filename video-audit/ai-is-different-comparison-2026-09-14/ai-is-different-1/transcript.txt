[0:00.00-0:06.00] Artificial intelligence is now performing tasks that were previously impossible for computers to handle.
[0:06.00-0:14.00] This shift in capability happens because the underlying logic of AI is distinct from the way standard software is built.

[0:18.00-0:22.00] A programmer writes explicit line-by-line instructions.
[0:22.00-0:28.00] At the core of these instructions are rigid rules, most commonly known as if-then-else statements.
[0:28.00-0:33.00] This flowchart shows exactly what those rules look like in practice for a user entering a password.
[0:33.00-0:35.00] The computer follows a strict path.

[0:49.00-0:54.00] You run the exact same process and you will get the exact same result every single time.
[0:54.00-0:56.00] AI abandons that approach.
[0:56.00-1:01.00] A programmer doesn't sit down and write the rules for every possible situation.
[1:01.00-1:04.00] This infographic illustrates the new process.
[1:04.00-1:08.00] Instead of written rules, AI relies on learned patterns.
[1:08.00-1:13.00] During the first step, training, the model ingests enormous amounts of data.

[1:17.00-1:20.00] It learns once before you even use it.
[1:20.00-1:25.00] When you ask the AI a question, it uses those learned patterns to generate an answer.
[1:25.00-1:30.00] Using probability, the model scores what the most likely next word should be.
[1:30.00-1:36.00] It makes a prediction, selects that single word, and then runs the entire cycle again to pick the word after that.
[1:36.00-1:40.00] Think of normal software like a robot following a recipe from a cookbook.
[1:40.00-1:45.00] Someone wrote every step, so the robot makes the exact same dish every time.
[1:45.00-1:50.00] AI is like a chef who cooked thousands of dishes and memorized the flavor profiles.
[1:50.00-1:54.00] No one handed it a cookbook, but it can still improvise a dish it has never made before.
[1:54.00-2:01.00] This shift from manual programming to learned patterns changes the basic architecture of the software.
[2:01.00-2:07.00] Instead of executing a sequence of steps, the computer is now navigating a field of probabilities.
[2:07.00-2:10.00] We can see this difference clearly with a practical test.

[2:32.00-2:35.00] AI relies on its probability engine.

[2:39.00-2:45.00] Ask it again, and it builds a fresh response based on learned patterns, suggesting NHL 26.
[2:45.00-2:48.00] Ask a third time, and it gives you God of War Ragnarok.
[2:48.00-2:52.00] Rules force the computer to repeat a programmed answer.
[2:52.00-2:57.00] Learned patterns allow it to dynamically construct a fresh response every time.
[2:57.00-3:01.00] Standard software also demands pristine organization.

[4:08.00-4:15.00] Because AI operates on probabilities rather than rigid rules, it introduces a major vulnerability.

[5:02.00-5:09.00] The architecture that allows AI to understand our world also makes its behavior unpredictable.
