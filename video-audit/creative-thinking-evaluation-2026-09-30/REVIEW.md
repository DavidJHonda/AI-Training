## Main findings

1. **Correct the AI-comparison narration at approximately 1:35–1:53.** “Everyone, using the same AI, gets similar answers” and “With the baseline equalized, the professional advantage moves entirely…” overstate the argument and differ from the current lesson. The lesson now explains that receiving an answer is only the beginning; the learner notices omissions, connects ideas, and chooses a direction. Keep that reasoning without asserting equal outputs or an exclusive source of advantage.
2. **Repair the accompanying diagram.** At 1:40 it visibly says “AI System: Rapid Homogenized Output” and “Identical Outputs (Zero Variance).” This is a stronger, misleading claim than the spoken “similar.” Model outputs can differ even between repeated requests; see [OpenAI’s documentation on nondeterministic outputs](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/advanced-usage). The diagram needs correction even if the spoken clause is cut. Do not automatically discard the later “Missing Angle” sequence: it conveys noticing a gap and filling it, although its labels could be simpler.

The latter plus the closing board makes about 60 seconds without a supporting scene. Narration actively teaches throughout, so these are not empty holds. Complete-card zooms make the active text readable. Relevant cutaways would provide visual variety without shortening explanations.
