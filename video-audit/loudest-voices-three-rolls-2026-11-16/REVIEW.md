## Exact language and listening limits

Version 2's transcript contains all four expert quotations, “Same field. Same evidence. Very different bets,” “None of them has a simple, one-sided view,” the ten-year line, the two-column takeaway, and both closing sentences. Its missing bridge words are **“So what’s a person to do? We think it helps to ask one more question.”** Versions 1 and 3 both paraphrase the hook line, the one-sided-view line, the ten-year line, the bridge and the two-column takeaway. Version 1 comes close on the expert quotations. Version 3 paraphrases all four. All three keep the retired history examples and the page activity out of the video.

I checked the lesson page, Markdown, production brief, all three complete machine transcripts, frame sequences from the uploads, and the installed v15 and review v16 visuals. Source comparison found no material conflict between the page and Markdown on the teaching covered here. Direct listening was unavailable. Speaker count, identity, name pronunciation, natural closing cadence, and seamless joins remain unverified. In particular, recognition repeatedly writes “warrior” for the lesson's “Worrier.” Spot-listen at 1:32 in version 1, 1:10 in version 2, and 1:04 in version 3 before treating that as a spoken error. The same caution applies to “thanks” in version 1. Graphics judgments use sampled frames, including closer samples across the bridge, rather than continuous playback.
