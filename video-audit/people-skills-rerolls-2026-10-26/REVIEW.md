### Roll 6 — not selected; needs substantial narration repair

The longest roll adds jersey descriptions to spoken narration, identifies number 4 as the interrupter, claims the project would lose points, turns the activity into credit for “saving their grade,” and substitutes broad claims about professional survival. “Clearly stung” removes the lesson's uncertainty about Maya's feelings. All four practices are present at a general level; these changes make it a worse base than 5.

### Roll 7 — useful donor; needs narration repair as a standalone

However, “Her idea deserves to be heard” omits “even if the group decides not to use it.” Its AI-future section compresses the actual reasons into performance baselines, collaboration, and psychological safety, losing the polished-work explanation and some trust/connection specifics. “The variable that determines how far you advance” overstates the claim.
