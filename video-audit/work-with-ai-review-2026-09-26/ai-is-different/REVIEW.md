## Findings

1. 0:00–0:13 and 3:57–4:13: superpowers and Kryptonite are not introduced in the explanations. They first arrive in the two closing lines. The Superman comparison is absent. This is a missing teaching connection, not merely a synonym preference.
2. 2:57–3:37: structured/unstructured data and the receipt/legal-pad examples are taught, but the range of inputs and outputs and “Available inputs and outputs depend on the app” are missing.
3. 3:40–3:57: it explains why normal software wins at exact, repeatable jobs. Add the positive counterpart: choose AI for messy, open-ended work; the student needs both halves of the decision.
4. 4:54–5:10: guardrails are described only as an app layer acting before processing. Restore both training to avoid harm and app safeguards, including their limits.
5. 0:44–0:49: “this software operates with absolute certainty” is broader than the password example supports. Keep the concrete claim about the same programmed rule returning the same result.
6. Preserve the password, robot/chef, three PS5 answers, receipt/legal-pad story, and all three risk explanations. These are the useful spine of a new pass.
7. Four long board appearances (26.5–46.5 seconds). Existing drawings at about 0:24 and 1:44–2:08 may support rules/learning breaks, but there is no verified story-specific donor for each risk card. Final break timings must follow the replacement narration.
