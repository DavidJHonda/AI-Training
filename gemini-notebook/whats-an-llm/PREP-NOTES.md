# What’s an LLM? — reroll preparation

Updated 2026-10-08 after review of the shortened candidate. This kit prepares a fresh full-lesson generation; it is not a continuation of the former two-part lesson.

The lesson moves from the app and its LLM engine, through patterns learned before use, to how the model uses the current words to build an answer one word at a time. Changing the supplied words changes what fits next; several words can fit even when the starting text stays the same.

## Teaching that must reach the narration

- Spell out Large Language Model and explain Large, Language, and Model. Preserve the app/engine line.
- Explain training before use and familiar patterns: jelly, star, time, never. Broaden to explaining ideas, asking questions, solving problems and misspellings.
- Speak both halves of the peanut butter/jelly versus peanut butter and banana/sandwich comparison. Explain the change, without percentages.
- Use the phone analogy and walk jelly → for → lunch, explaining how each word updates the input.
- Explicitly say that more than one word can fit, so the same starting text can lead to different answers. A single sequence does not teach variability.
- End with the exact two closing lines. The interactive story activities stay on the page and are not narrated.

The context principle and variability sentence are now required verbatim audio and stand alone in the Markdown. These were the gaps in the shortened candidate; the video must preserve the understanding provided by the reading.
