This prompt forms our baseline. We can look at exactly what is expected of you before any AI is introduced. We can break this workflow into two distinct stages. Stage one is routine work, learning what customers are saying. Before AI, you sit down and read all 500 reviews yourself. You group the common complaints, build a spreadsheet, and write a summary. With AI, the execution shifts. The system organizes those reviews and drafts a summary for you. Your job becomes spot checking the results and investigating anything that looks inaccurate. Spending less time on the routine sorting means you have more time for high-level judgment and decision-making, and how well you perform that second stage relies entirely on what you already know. When we talk about AI altering these workflows, the assistance falls into two distinct categories. Automate and augment. Automation happens when AI takes over a step entirely.
