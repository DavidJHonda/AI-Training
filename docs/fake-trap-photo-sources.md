# Fake Trap TRY IT: image sources

This activity uses three camera photographs supplied by the course owner and three images created with the built-in image-generation tool for this exercise. Public feedback omits the photographer’s name, as requested. A/B filenames avoid giving away the answer in the visible image labels or alt text.

| Pair | A | B |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cat | AI-generated | User-supplied pet photograph |
| Sculpture | User photograph taken in Liverpool, England | AI-generated |
| Venice | AI-generated | User photograph taken in Venice, Italy |

The Liverpool photograph was cropped to its top 1290 × 1364 pixels, ending below the rectangular torso opening and above the main figure’s groin. No generative edits were made to any camera photograph. The originals were resized as needed and encoded as JPGs for the site; the original attachments remain unchanged.

All six current assets live in `course-assets/fake-trap/`, with names `fake-trap-try-{cat,sculpture,venice}-{a,b}.jpg`. They belong to the on-page TRY IT only. Video prep intentionally excludes interactive activities, so this does not change the lesson’s video materials.

Generation briefs: a casual fluffy-cat smartphone photograph on a sofa; a bronze sculpture containing smaller figures outside a gallery; and a nighttime Venetian bridge viewed from a boat with a deck reflection. All were generated as new scenes with plausible natural lighting and ordinary travel/snapshot quality, not alterations of the supplied photographs.
