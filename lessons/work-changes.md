## NAVIGATE THE FUTURE

# Work Changes

Before you know it, you’ll be finished with school and starting your career. How is AI changing work? Glad you asked.

Familiar job titles will still be around, like entrepreneur, teacher, and lawyer. But what those jobs look like is already changing, including what people do each day.

Keep two ideas in mind. First, you already know AI is best at specific things, and that’s where you’ll likely use it most in your career. Second, what will set you apart is what you already know. Now put those ideas together on your first assignment at work.

### Board 1: Your First Assignment

**Image file:** `../course-assets/work-changes/work-changes-assignment-short.jpg`

![Your First Assignment](../course-assets/work-changes/work-changes-assignment-short.jpg)

**Teaching content:**

Your first assignment.

Your manager tells you, “We’re getting bad customer feedback on our new product. Read the last 500 reviews and tell us what’s happening and how we should fix it.”

**Stage 1: Detail Work.**

**Without AI:** You read all 500 reviews, group the complaints, build the spreadsheet, and write a summary.

**With AI:** AI produces a first pass. You check it against the reviews and correct mistakes.

**Stage 2: Analysis & Recommendation.**

**Without AI:** You investigate the causes, compare fixes, and present your recommendation.

**With AI:** AI helps you explore causes and compare fixes. You evaluate its suggestions and make the recommendation.

**Same assignment. Same responsibility. A different way to do the work.**

## Do You See the Point?

With AI’s help, you can spend less time organizing information and more time investigating what it means and deciding what to do. Checking and improving AI’s work takes what you already know.

## The Concepts

You just saw two ways to use AI at work: automate and augment.

### Board 2: Two Ways AI Changes the Work

**Image file:** `work-changes-automation-and-augmentation.jpg`

![Two Ways AI Changes the Work](work-changes-automation-and-augmentation.jpg)

**Teaching content:**

Two ways AI changes the work.

Automate. AI takes over a step. It sorted the reviews, grouped the ideas, and created the first summary.

Augment. AI helps a person do more. AI helped you explore more explanations, compare more options, and improve your recommendation.

The work still has your name on it. You own the outcome.

## The Part That Matters

AI is already changing some entry-level work. Basic research, starter code, and simple analysis often helped new graduates learn a profession. AI can now handle or assist with some of that work.

AI can make you productive before it makes you knowledgeable.

That creates a strange problem. You might be asked to check sophisticated work before you’ve had the chance to learn it.

You already know what you must do. Learn. And learn more. And you’re in the best place in the world to do it: school.

## Closing Message

AI is changing the work you’ll do.

Now it’s on you to LEARN.
