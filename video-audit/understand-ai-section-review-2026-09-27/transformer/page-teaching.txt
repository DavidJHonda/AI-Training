WHY THIS WAS HARD FOR AI
Our human brains see the right meaning instantly from the surrounding words. For earlier AI, this was a challenge because it read text in order, one word at a time. In longer passages, it could lose track of the earlier words.
How Earlier AI Read Text. The diagram follows the full sentence The cat sat on the mat during the May rainstorm because it was tired. We know IT refers to CAT. Earlier AI often struggled to keep that connection, especially in longer passages.

One catch: word order
Reading everything at once creates a problem that reading in order never had. Consider a simple sentence: \u201cDog bites man.\u201d Same three tokens. Same starting embeddings. But the order carries the meaning.
