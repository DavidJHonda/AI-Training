Core idea: curiosity helps students notice useful changes; flexibility helps them test and adopt only what works.

Open on a fictional school basketball team. A play worked all season, then another team learned how to stop it. The answer is not to keep forcing the old play. Be curious enough to look for a better one and flexible enough to stop relying on what used to work.

Apply the idea to AI. Models, tools, and workflows change quickly, but students do not need to chase every feature. They need a simple way to notice changes that matter and evaluate them.
