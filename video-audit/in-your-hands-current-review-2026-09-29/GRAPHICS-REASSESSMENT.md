## Revised conclusion

**Retain both existing Notebook opening scenes. Preserve the approved column walk. The long board sequence remains the main visual improvement opportunity, but a fixed schedule of custom stills is not the default solution.**

The new rule prioritizes what a scene teaches, including its animation, sequence, emphasis, and consistent added examples. Independent supporting diagrams are not course-board recreations merely because they teach the same idea. Illustrative numbers are not automatically errors; misleading claims are. Custom artwork follows evaluation of useful existing scenes and targeted repairs, rather than replacing effective animation for polish.

This supersedes the earlier report's six-cutaway schedule as a recommended production plan. Those timestamps remain possible editorial windows, not a requirement to insert six pictures or keep every board exposure below exactly twenty seconds. Section 8b still calls for supporting scenes during extended board runs; its approximate duration guidance does not justify irrelevant or disruptive filler.
