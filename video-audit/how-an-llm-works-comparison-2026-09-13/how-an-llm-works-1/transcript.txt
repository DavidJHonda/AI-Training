[0:05.90-0:12.60] Hidden underneath, a large language model, or LLM, serves as the engine doing the actual work.
[0:12.60-0:18.90] The large, in that name, refers to the colossal amounts of text and code absorbed during its creation.
[0:18.90-0:24.50] The language component represents its capacity to read, summarize, and explain information.

[0:31.60-0:37.60] Here, the app serves as the user-facing interface, while the LLM acts as the power source.
[0:37.60-0:44.30] When an AI generates a sentence, it relies on a predictive process rather than a database of facts or meanings.
[0:44.30-0:46.90] This process happens in two phases.
[0:46.90-0:52.00] First, the model undergoes training, absorbing patterns from massive data sets.
[0:52.00-0:56.40] This learn once phase converts text into numerical patterns.
[0:56.40-1:02.00] Then, for every word, the model calculates the probability of what should come next and loops.
[1:02.00-1:05.40] Every answer uses those stored initial patterns.
[1:05.40-1:10.00] Training refines the model through a continuous cycle of guessing and checking.

[1:33.00-1:34.40] Peanut butter and jelly.
[1:34.40-1:39.50] Because the sequence appears so often in the training data, the AI internalizes it.

[2:48.90-2:54.00] A full paragraph runs this loop hundreds of times, fast enough to look like thought.
