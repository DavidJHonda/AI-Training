## Source QA and fact-check notes

No material correction to the current lesson was identified. The narration/prep comparison did reveal meaningful drift in roll 2, independently of its visuals.

- [AlphaFold Database](https://www.alphafold.ebi.ac.uk/about) supports freely available predictions and their role in investigating disease and new treatments. It does not justify turning this into a universal prerequisite for designing every medicine or saying AI physically folds proteins inside cells.
- [Lund University's MASAI results](https://www.lunduniversity.lu.se/article/ai-support-breast-cancer-screening-fewer-missed-cancer-cases) support the lesson's broad trial comparison and report 106,000 participants. Its current report gives 29% greater detection, so roll 2's implied 28% and roll 3's 20% should not be imported as the result of the current lesson's specified trial without reconciling study phase and denominator. Preserve the qualitative comparison; do not call every illustrative bar chart invalid.
- [Google's flood-forecasting research](https://research.google/pubs/ai-increases-global-access-to-reliable-flood-forecasts/) supports free forecasts in ungauged areas and earlier warnings, not guaranteed safety or an exact five days in every situation. [Its model explanation](https://research.google/blog/using-ai-to-expand-global-access-to-reliable-flood-forecasts/) describes historical and forecast weather inputs, including gauge-based precipitation data. That is why roll 2's “math rather than sensors” explanation is misleading; lacking a local river gauge does not mean a system runs without measured data.
